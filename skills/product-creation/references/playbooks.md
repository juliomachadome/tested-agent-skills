# Playbooks: product creation

## 1. Build-mode decision worksheet
Fill in one line each, then pick the mode.
1. Signal: paid (presale, deposit) / replies / likes only.
2. Window: days left before the wave or date fades.
3. Price planned and who pays.
4. Risk: refunds, reputation, public promise (low / mid / high).
5. Hours to build per mode versus expected sales in the window. Expected gross = audience reached x conversion you can defend (use your own past rate; a cold creator rate for a first product is often well under 2% of reached viewers **[F]**) x price.
Rule: start fast unless two or more of these are true: paid signal is strong, window above one month, price above about R$200, risk high. Then step one mode up, never two.
**Worked example**: creator, viral series, 150,000 followers, 7 days. Signal: likes and comments (no paid signal). Window: about 7-10 days. Price: R$67 (any currency works; same arithmetic). Risk: low to mid. Hours available: 15. A reach of 15% of followers = 22,500 people; at 1% conversion = 225 sales x R$67 = R$15,075 gross before fees; at 0.3% = 67 sales x R$67 = R$4,489. Fast mode: a 30-page PDF or Notion kit plus a presale page needs about 12 hours; standard (course) needs 60+ hours and misses the window. Choose fast; presale by day 2; deliver by day 7; run the phase review at 50 buyers.

## 2. 48-hour presale launch
Hour 0-4: write the one-sentence promise, the contents list (what, format, date of delivery), price, refund terms. Hour 4-12: build the sale page (copywriting) and checkout on a platform that handles tax and delivery; set a real delivery date. Hour 12-24: tell the warm list first (viral-content, storytelling for the story); offer the early price only if it truly ends. Hour 24-48: reply to every question (note them: they become the next version); say honestly what is not included. Stop rule at hour 48: fewer than a floor you set in advance (for example 10 buyers) means ask five non-buyers why, do not build more. Then deliver exactly what was promised, on the date, with a welcome message and one first step.

## 3. Phase-review checklist
Run at a fixed count or date (50 buyers, or 14 days after delivery).
- Sales vs plan (units, revenue, by source).
- Refund rate by order cohort and reasons.
- Repeated requests (count of buyers asking the same thing).
- Margin: price minus platform fee, payment fee, tax, support time x hourly value.
- Support load per 100 buyers and top three topics.
- Completion or first-value rate.
Decide one: improve (name one change), step up the mode (name the data that asks), add the next rung (ladder test below), or stop (`5`). Write the decision, owner and next review date.

## 4. Product ladder design
Order by commitment: free or tiny entry, low-ticket kit, mid-ticket course or cohort, high-ticket mentoring or done-for-you, recurring (community, membership, SaaS). Add a rung only when: (a) the rung below paid for itself, (b) at least a few buyers asked for the next step in their own words, (c) delivery capacity exists. Each rung solves the next problem of the same buyer; price jump about 3-5x between rungs **[F]**. Test before building: ask finishers, then presell to the warm list. Recurring only if value repeats monthly (new content, community, tool use); otherwise it churns. Price, bumps and guarantee go to offers; the economics to business-model-finance.

## 5. Kill or continue
Set before launch: sales floor, refund ceiling, hour budget. At review score each: met / near / missed.
- All met or near: continue and improve.
- Sales missed, refunds fine: change the promise or the audience once, test again; stop after the second miss.
- Refunds missed: fix promise and onboarding once (diagnosis tree 3); a repeat means stop selling it.
- Margin negative after support: raise price, cut support, or stop.
Stop cleanly: close sales, keep delivering to buyers as promised, refund when owed, archive learnings. Sunk cost is not an argument; hours already spent do not change the future return.
**Worked example (next rung)**: a R$47 kit sold 380 units = R$17,860 gross. Platform at 8.99% + R$2.49 per sale (one platform's page on 18/11/2026; check yours) takes R$2,552 and leaves about R$15,308. Refunds 19 = 5%. Of 152 finishers, 41 asked for a guided version (27%, measured among finishers). Take rate guess for a R$297 cohort: assume 25% convert = about 10 buyers = about R$3,044; delivery 8 weeks x 3 h = 24 h. Cheap test first: presell 10 seats to the 41 askers with a 7-day close; if 10 pay, run it; if 3 pay, do a cheaper intermediate rung (live workshop R$97). Then add the rung with the checkpoint above.
