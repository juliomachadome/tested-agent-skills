# Product types: comparison and notes

Figures are orders of magnitude (**[F]**); replace with your own. Margin is before platform and payment fees and your time.

| Type | Cost to start | Time to first sale | Margin | Delivery | Main risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple digital (PDF, ebook, template, project folder, Notion) | near zero | hours to 2 days | 85-95% | instant, platform | copying, low perceived value, refunds on weak promise |
| Course (recorded) | low to mid (recording, editing) | 1-4 weeks (presale in days) | 85-95% | platform | low completion, refunds, update burden |
| Cohort or mentoring | low | days (presale) | high, capped by hours | live, your time | capacity, scheduling, reputation |
| Community | low | days | recurring, churn | platform | moderation load, churn, empty room |
| Done-for-you service | none | days | high per job, not scalable | your time | scope creep, late delivery |
| No-code tool or app | mid | 2-8 weeks | high at scale | web or store | bugs, support, platform fees |
| SaaS | high | months | high at scale | web | churn, security, maintenance |
| Print on demand | low | days | 10-40% | partner prints, ships | quality, delivery delays, returns |
| Dropshipping | low | days | 10-30% | supplier ships | long shipping, returns, chargebacks |
| Own stock | highest (cash) | weeks | 30-60% | you ship | stock risk, invoices, returns |

## Notes per type
**Simple digital.** Best first product: sell it fast, deliver in minutes, learn from questions. Include a start page, one quick win, an update promise. Versioning: v1.0, changelog.
**Content and service.** Cohort and mentoring presell well because delivery is later; cap seats to your capacity. Courses need onboarding and a nudge sequence; keep modules short. Done-for-you is a good source of proof and requests before productising.
**Software.** Validate by concierge or a no-code wrapper before building; discovery, PRD and roadmap go to product-management; app store listing, review and release go to mobile-app-engineering; onboarding flows to ux-design (or an onboarding skill if installed); subscription tiers and plan price to offers and business-model-finance.
**Physical.** Costs include product, shipping, packaging, returns, tax and invoices (issue the fiscal document per your tax regime and country; ask an accountant). Regulated categories need certification or registration before sale: for example INMETRO or ANVISA in Brazil (the ANVISA regulation page lists subject areas such as cosmetics, saneantes, foods and medical devices; the INMETRO page read carried no product lists), CE or UKCA marking in Europe and the UK, FDA or CPSC rules in the US. General information only: check the official pages of the country where you sell and a professional there. Distance-sale consumer rights depend on the buyer's country (`launch-and-quality.md` 5; customer-support).

## Delivery platforms
Choose by the buyers' country, the payment methods they use, the fee at your volume, delivery needs, launch speed and who collects tax (`launch-and-quality.md` 7).
- **Kiwify, Hotmart (Brazil)**: checkout with Pix, card and boleto, affiliates, delivery and member areas. Kiwify's landing page showed 8.99% + R$2.49 per sale on 18/11/2026; Hotmart's page showed a 9.9% + R$2.49 base rate with lower tiers by monthly revenue; fees change, read the live page.
- **Gumroad (global)**: simple; its pricing page showed 10% + $0.50 on direct sales and 30% on sales found through its Discover marketplace (18/11/2026).
- **Merchant-of-record platforms (global)**: sell to the buyer and collect and remit VAT or sales tax for you, handling refunds and chargebacks (Lemon Squeezy documents this model, but its help page says it stopped new sign-ups and will shut down on 31 March 2027; Paddle and similar services work this way **[F]**, verify availability in your country).
- **Stripe**: own checkout and subscriptions, full control; you handle delivery, tax, invoices and support. Its Checkout docs also list Stripe Managed Payments, described as Stripe handling sales-tax and VAT obligations in more than 80 countries plus customer support and disputes (check eligibility and terms). Brazil page showed 3.99% + R$0.39 for domestic cards, 1.19% for Pix and R$3.45 for boleto on 18/11/2026; other countries differ.
- **Shopify and similar**: store for physical goods, with its own app and payment fees **[F]**.
- **Own site**: most control and brand, most work and risk (payments, security).
- **App stores**: review time, store fees and rules apply (mobile-app-engineering).
