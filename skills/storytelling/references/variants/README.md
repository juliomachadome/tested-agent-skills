# Variants: selector by medium and goal

Pick the file by medium, then goal. Core craft stays in `../../SKILL.md`; templates in `../playbooks.md`; symptoms in `../diagnosis-playbook.md`.

| Medium | Goal | Structure to start with | Open |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60-120 s video, AI-assisted | Entertain, grow an audience, tease a brand | Story spine compressed to 5-7 scenes | `short-form-ai.md` |
| Episodic series, interactive or voted | Keep viewers across episodes, build a community | Engine plus season arc, per-episode beat | `series-interactive.md` |
| Brand page, campaign, founder story | Build trust, explain why you exist | Customer as hero, company as guide | `brand-marketing.md` |
| Pitch deck, keynote, sales presentation | Get a decision | Problem, stakes, solution, proof, ask | `pitch-presentation.md` |
| Report, dashboard review, board update | Explain one finding | One insight, context, contrast | `data-storytelling.md` |
| Short post, caption, anecdote in a talk | Make a point memorable | Story spine in 4 lines | `../playbooks.md` section 1 |
| Film, novel, long form | Craft a full arc | Three acts plus beat sheet | `../playbooks.md` section 2 |

Rules of thumb: more length allows more characters and subplots; less length demands one want, one obstacle, one change. If a piece needs distribution analysis (hooks by metric, posting cadence), switch to `skills/viral-content`. If it is final sales copy, switch to copywriting; ad specs, ad-creatives.

## How to use the selector
1. Fill Step 0 in `../diagnosis-playbook.md` (goal, audience, medium, length, end state). The row you pick must match the medium and the verb of the end state.
2. Open the variant, draft with the template named there, then run the revision checklist (`../playbooks.md` 9).
3. If the draft fails, find the symptom in `../diagnosis-playbook.md` before rewriting sentences.

## Common mixes
- A brand series on short video: `series-interactive.md` for the engine and `brand-marketing.md` for the customer-as-hero rule; keep claims provable.
- A pitch with a data core: `pitch-presentation.md` for the arc, `data-storytelling.md` for the one insight and contrast.
- An AI-made episode in a vote-driven series: `short-form-ai.md` for scenes and disclosure, `series-interactive.md` for votes and the bible.
Do not open all files for one job; read the one that matches and follow its links.
Interactive or voted pieces carry extra duties: disclose how choices are counted and keep the central question under editorial control.
