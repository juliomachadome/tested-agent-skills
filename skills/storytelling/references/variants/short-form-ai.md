# Short-form, AI-assisted story (60-120 seconds)

At 60-120 seconds you can afford one want, one obstacle, one change. Evidence tags as in `SKILL.md`.

## 1. Build in scenes
Target 5-8 scenes of 8-20 seconds each (60 s: 5 scenes; 120 s: 8). Plan:
1. Hook (0-3 s): the moment of change or danger; a question or an image the audience cannot place.
2. Setup in one line: who wants what.
3. Obstacle 1: attempt fails.
4. Obstacle 2 or reversal: cost rises.
5. Low point: the want seems lost.
6. Turn: the lead acts on what they learned.
7. Payoff and change: image rather than explanation.
A voiceover of about 2.2 words per second gives roughly 130 words for 60 s; confirm by reading aloud. Subtitles carry the key lines; many viewers watch muted.

## 2. Scene-by-scene prompt discipline
Write each scene as: location, who, action (verb), emotion, the one detail, camera, length. Keep character descriptions identical across scenes (a character sheet pasted each time). One action per shot; AI video tends to fail on complex multi-step action, text, hands and long continuity, so cut around them.

## 3. AI script pitfalls
- Generic names, "In a world where", and a closing moral: ban them in the prompt.
- Summary scenes (the lead "learns to believe in herself"): require a visible action.
- Over-neat resolution: ask for a cost.
- Same rhythm in every sentence: edit by hand for voice; read aloud.
- Invented facts, quotes or "real" users: do not present as real; label composites and fiction.
- Likeness and voice of real people without consent: do not.

## 4. Disclosure and rights
Label AI-generated or synthetic content as the platform and law require (platform AI labels; EU AI Act transparency for synthetic media as it applies); check current rules per platform and country. Music, voices and images need licences; AI outputs may carry limited protection. State what you did not verify.

## 5. Check before shipping
Run the revision checklist (`../playbooks.md` 9). Then the 30-second stranger test. Distribution questions (thumbnail, posting time, metrics) belong to `skills/viral-content` and ad specs to ad-creatives.

## 6. Worked example: 60 seconds, six scenes
Logline: a night-shift baker must finish 200 loaves before the 5 a.m. delivery, but the oven fails at 3 a.m. Scene 1 (0-5 s): the oven dies in close-up, steam stops; question: will the bread be ready? Scene 2: she checks the clock, 200 raw loaves in rows. Scene 3: she tries her phone, no one answers. Scene 4 (low point): she sits on a flour sack; a delivery driver arrives early. Scene 5 (turn): she opens the old stone oven in the back that she swore she would never use. Scene 6: sunrise, the van leaves with loaves; her hands are burned, the old oven stays lit. Change: she accepts what she avoided. Voiceover minimal; subtitles carry the clock.

## 7. Edit pass for AI output
Replace default names and places with chosen ones, delete adjectives that explain feelings, cut any last line that states the moral, make one scene wordless, and vary sentence length. Re-run the 30-second stranger test on the edited version.
