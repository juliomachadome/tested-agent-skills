# Prioritisation notes

How to run the session, when to pick each tool and a worked cost-of-delay example: `playbooks.md` 4. Symptoms (HiPPO, 400-item backlog, sales one-offs): `diagnosis-playbook.md`. Evidence tags: **[M]** serious but single, **[F]** vendor or practitioner.

- **RICE** (popularised by Intercom, vendor practitioner): Reach (people or events per period), Impact (3 massive, 2 high, 1 medium, 0.5 low, 0.25 minimal), Confidence (100, 80, 50 percent), Effort (person-months). Score = R x I x C / E. Use ranges, not point values; sort, then discuss outliers. Worked example in `diagnosis-playbook.md`. **[F]**
- **ICE**: impact, confidence, ease scored 1-10 and averaged; fast and coarse, fine for small teams and growth ideas, not for comparing across teams. **[F]**
- **Kano** (Kano et al., 1984): classify by asking functional and dysfunctional question pairs; categories must-be, performance (one-dimensional), attractive (delighter), indifferent, reverse. Must-be features cost you when missing and earn little when present; delighters become expected over time, so re-survey. **[M]**
- **Opportunity solution tree** (Torres): outcome at top, opportunities beneath, multiple solutions per opportunity, assumption tests per solution; compare solutions side by side to avoid single-idea fixation. **[M]**
- **Cost of delay and WSJF** (Reinertsen; SAFe): relative cost of delay (value, time criticality, risk reduction or opportunity enablement) divided by relative job size. Needs defensible numbers; if the costs are invented, use a simpler method. **[M]** for the idea, **[F]** for the SAFe scoring.
- **Sanity checks:** Does the top item serve the stated outcome? Is the effort estimate from the people who build it? What did we assume about adoption? What is the smallest slice that would teach us? What happens if we do nothing, or buy or reuse?
- Technical debt and reliability compete honestly: express them as risk or capacity cost, not as "engineering wishes".
- Keep a "not doing" list with reasons and the evidence that would change the decision.
