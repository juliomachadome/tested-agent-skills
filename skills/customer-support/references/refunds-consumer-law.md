# Refunds, cancellation, complaints: orientation, not legal advice

Checked 15/11/2026. Each rule below carries a status: **[P]** = article number and gist read on the official text this pass (Brazil: normas.leg.br, see section 3); **[unv]** = from prior knowledge, not read, so numbers and details must be checked before use in a policy or a customer reply. Sources and statuses: `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`. Law wins over company policy; when unsure, take the customer-friendly reading, tell the owner, and ask counsel. Statements are restated, not copied.

## 1. How to use this in a ticket (decision order)
1. Which law applies? Customer's country of residence for consumers (EU rules are mandatory for EU consumers even if the seller is elsewhere and targets them) **[unv]**; the contract's own terms second.
2. What is the contract type? Goods, digital content, service, subscription, in-person versus distance.
3. Is a statutory right open (withdrawal window, conformity remedy, cancellation)? If yes, grant it; do not ask "why" as a condition.
4. If no statutory right: apply the written company policy; if the policy is silent, state the assumption and flag it.
5. Record: date, which right or policy, decision, who approved. Patterns from these records feed `diagnosis-playbook.md` (refund rate branch).

## 2. European Union
- **Withdrawal, Directive 2011/83/EU [P]**: art. 9(1): 14 days to withdraw from distance or off-premises contracts, no reason needed. Art. 9(2): for services the period starts at conclusion of the contract; for goods when the consumer (or a named non-carrier third party) takes physical possession; digital content not on a tangible medium also counts from conclusion. Art. 10: if the trader never gave the required withdrawal information, the period extends up to 12 months after the normal one ends. Art. 13: refund of all payments, including standard delivery, without undue delay and within 14 days of learning of the withdrawal, normally by the original payment method; the trader may hold the refund until goods are back or proof of return is given. Art. 14(1): consumer returns goods within 14 days after notifying and normally bears only the direct return cost, unless the trader agreed to bear it or failed to say so.
- **Services started inside the window, art. 14(3) [P]**: if the consumer asked for performance to begin during the withdrawal period and then withdraws, they owe an amount proportionate to what was already provided, calculated on the contract price (market value if the price is excessive).
- **Digital content exception, art. 16(m) [P]**: the right does not apply to digital content not on a tangible medium once performance has begun with the consumer's prior express consent **and** their acknowledgement that the right is lost. Art. 8(7) [P]: the trader must send the contract confirmation on a durable medium that includes that consent confirmation. Practical reading: a pre-ticked box or a line buried in terms is a weak basis; the checkout needs a separate, unticked consent and acknowledgement, stored with timestamp. If the evidence is missing, treat the 14 days as open and refund. (Whether the exception also covers digital services such as SaaS differs: in the text read, 16(m) is about content; for services use art. 14(3).)
- **Withdrawal function (button), Directive (EU) 2023/2673 [P]**: inserts art. 11a: for distance contracts concluded through an online interface the trader must offer a withdrawal function labelled "withdraw from contract here" or an unambiguous equivalent, prominent and easy to reach for the whole withdrawal period; the consumer gives name, contract identification and a means to receive confirmation; a final step labelled "confirm withdrawal" or equivalent; the trader promptly sends an acknowledgement on a durable medium with content and date and time; withdrawal counts as exercised if submitted before the period ends. **Dates read on EUR-Lex: transposition by 19 December 2025; applies from 19 June 2026.** As of 15/11/2026 the rule is therefore due in force; national transposition laws in each Member State were **not checked**. Support consequence: a customer who used the button has withdrawn, no further justification or "confirm by e-mail" loop; and a withdrawal made by e-mail or chat is still valid evidence of exercise **[unv: national forms]**.
- **Conformity remedies, Directive (EU) 2019/771 (goods) [P]**: art. 10: seller liable for lack of conformity existing at delivery that becomes apparent within two years (Member States may set longer; shorter agreed periods only for second-hand goods, not below one year); art. 11: lack of conformity appearing within one year is presumed to have existed at delivery (Member States may use two years); art. 13: repair or replacement, proportionate price reduction, or termination; termination barred if the lack of conformity is minor, burden on the seller to show that. **Directive (EU) 2019/770 (digital content and services) [S]**: only the recitals were read: conformity remedies of bringing into conformity, price reduction or termination, with liability for at least two years for single supply and for the contract duration for continuous supply; operative article numbers **not read, unverified**.
- **Data-subject requests, GDPR [P]**: art. 12(3): answer within one month of receipt, extendable by two further months where necessary given complexity and number of requests (the data subject must be told of the extension); art. 15(1) access; art. 17(1) erasure on listed grounds. Support rule: tag and forward the same day to the privacy owner; verify identity proportionately (art. 12 text on identity beyond what was read: **unv**); never answer from memory.
- **Not read: [unv]** unfair commercial practices rules on cancellation obstacles, national automatic-renewal rules, consumer-ADR and ODR platform duties.

## 3. Brazil [P]
Read 15/11/2026 on normas.leg.br (Câmara consolidated text for the CDC; original publication for the decrees). Planalto was unreachable, so the planalto.gov.br links in `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` are alternatives only. Text restated, not copied.
- **Lei 8.078/1990 (CDC), art. 49 [P]**: 7 days to desist ("arrependimento"), counted from signing the contract or from receiving the product or service, when the contract is made outside the commercial establishment; the text names phone and door-to-door sales as examples and does **not** say "internet" (online sales come in through Decreto 7.962, below). Sole paragraph: everything paid, under any title, during that period is returned at once with monetary correction (so shipping paid is covered by the wording). No reason is required.
- **Defects ("vícios")**: art. 26: the right to complain about apparent or easily found defects lapses (caduca) in 30 days for services and non-durable goods, 90 days for services and durable goods, counted from actual delivery or the end of the service (art. 26 par. 1); a hidden defect counts from when it shows (par. 3); a documented complaint to the supplier stops the clock until a clear negative answer (par. 2 I). Art. 18 (goods): if the defect is not fixed within 30 days the customer may choose replacement, immediate refund with monetary correction (plus damages), or proportional price cut; the parties may agree a different fixing period of 7 to 180 days (in adhesion contracts, in a separate clause the customer signs), and the customer may skip the wait when replacing parts would harm the product, reduce its value or it is essential (par. 3). Art. 20 (services): customer chooses between redoing the service, immediate refund with monetary correction, or proportional price cut, with no 30-day wait. Art. 35: if the supplier refuses to honour an offer or advertisement, customer may demand performance, accept an equivalent, or rescind with refund and damages. Art. 42 sole paragraph: an amount unduly charged is returned at double, plus correction and interest, unless the error was justifiable.
- **Decreto 7.962/2013 (e-commerce) [P]**: art. 2: the site must show, prominently, the supplier's legal name and tax number, physical and electronic address, essential features of the product, extra costs such as delivery or insurance itemised in the price, full offer conditions and any restriction. Art. 4: contract summary before purchase, tools to spot and fix input errors, immediate confirmation of receipt of the order acceptance, contract available in a storable form right after purchase, an effective electronic support channel for information, doubt, complaint, suspension or cancellation (support must confirm receipt at once, in the same medium) and an answer **within 5 days** (sole paragraph; the decree's text gives no "working days" qualifier). Art. 5: the customer exercises arrependimento in the same tool used to buy, without prejudice to other means; ancillary contracts end at no cost; the supplier tells the card issuer at once so the charge is not billed or is reversed; and sends immediate confirmation of the request. Art. 7: breach brings the CDC art. 56 sanctions.
- **Decreto 11.034/2022 (SAC) [P]**: scope is customer service ("SAC") of services regulated by the federal Executive (art. 1), not purchase or contracting itself (art. 2 sole paragraph), so it is not a general rule for every online shop. Art. 3: free of charge. Art. 4: access 24 hours, 7 days a week through at least one integrated channel, phone mandatory; no personal data required before reaching the agent (par. 4); no advertising while waiting without prior consent (par. 5). Art. 5: phone service for at least 8 hours a day with a human, first menu must include complaint and cancellation options, and regulators set the **maximum wait** to reach an agent and to be transferred; **the decree itself states no number of seconds**, so that figure sits in the sector regulator's rules (not read). Art. 10: no asking the customer to repeat the request once registered. Art. 12: customer can follow every request by a numeric record; history sent within 5 calendar days of request; phone recordings kept at least 90 days; service records kept at least 2 years after resolution. Art. 13: answer within **7 calendar days** of registration, clear, objective and conclusive, covering every point; for an unrequested service or undue charge, suspend the charge immediately (par. 3); regulators may set a resolution deadline. Art. 14: cancellation can be requested by any means available for buying; effects are immediate (except where technical processing is needed, with a regulator-set deadline); the customer is told of exit conditions and minimum-term fines; proof of the request is sent; scheduled cancellation only with consent. Art. 16: breach brings CDC art. 56 sanctions. Entry into force 180 days after publication (art. 18).
- Practical default for Brazilian customers: treat a request within 7 days of an online purchase as a statutory withdrawal and refund in full including shipping; do not ask for a reason. Whether other sector regulators' rules apply to your product is **[unv]**: ask counsel.

## 4. United States
- **FTC Negative Option Rule ("click to cancel") [P, partly]**: the FTC page opened this pass says the FTC announced the amended rule on 16 October 2024, published 15 November 2024 (16 CFR Part 425); a stay request was denied on 13 December 2024; the Commission later published (12 February 2026) a notice titled as a revision "to conform these rules to federal court decisions"; and on 11 March 2026 issued an advance notice of proposed rulemaking (docket FTC-2026-0265, Federal Register notice 13 March 2026) seeking comment on rules to help consumers avoid unwanted recurring charges and cancel without obstacles. **Honest status: the 2024 amendments are not in force**. The page did not mention the 2025 Eighth Circuit vacatur itself; that the 2024 amendments were vacated by a court in 2025 is from the brief and prior knowledge **[unv: court, date]**, consistent with the later "conform to court decisions" notice. A new rule is only at the pre-proposal stage.
- **Still relevant [unv]**: the older Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act (clear disclosure, express informed consent, simple cancellation for online negative-option sales) and state automatic-renewal laws (several states have their own cancellation, notice and reminder rules). The FTC ROSCA guidance page returned 404 this pass.
- Safe design anywhere: cancel in the same channel and about the same number of steps as sign-up; disclose price, renewal and cancellation terms before charging; send a reminder before annual renewals **[F]**.

## 5. Card disputes (chargebacks), basics **[F/unv]**
Network documents read in a browser 15/11/2026: Visa Core Rules (public PDF, edition Oct 2026) give per-condition dispute time limits, e.g. 13.1 Merchandise/Services Not Received and 13.6 Credit Not Processed: no later than 120 calendar days (issuer waits 15 days first); Visa monitoring programmes are VFDMP (10.4.2) and VAMP (10.4.3.1). The Mastercard Chargeback Guide (Merchant Edition, 13 May 2025) covers chargeback, second presentment, arbitration and compliance cases; credit-adjustment disputes run 1-120 calendar days after settlement. **Ratio thresholds, other reason codes and any newer edition are unverified**; take them from the acquirer or payment provider's documentation.
- A chargeback is the cardholder's bank reversing a charge; the merchant gets the money pulled back plus often a fee, and can submit evidence once ("representment").
- Typical categories: fraud or unrecognised charge, "product or service not received", "not as described", duplicate, subscription cancelled but charged, credit not processed.
- Evidence that wins: clear order and delivery or access logs, the cancellation or refund policy shown at checkout, the customer's consent record, and the support thread showing you answered. Evidence that loses: a policy shown only after payment, no log of access.
- Support action: a refund is almost always cheaper than a dispute (fee plus a hit on the dispute ratio). If a customer says "I will call my bank", issue the refund you owe at once and say so; never threaten, never hold a refund hostage to a dispute being withdrawn **[F]**. A high dispute ratio can lead to higher fees or loss of the merchant account (thresholds vary, unverified).

## 6. Complaints and cancellations: the shape that works everywhere
Acknowledge fast; investigate; answer in writing with the reasoning; name the next escalation path (internal escalation, then ADR, ombudsman or regulator where one exists); keep the thread. Cancellation: confirm the date it takes effect, what is refunded or owed, what happens to data (deletion or export, see `playbooks.md` privacy recipe), and that no further charge will occur. Offer a pause or downgrade once, with no pressure, then execute.
