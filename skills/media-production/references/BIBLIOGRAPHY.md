## Added 18/11/2026 (platform label pages)
22. Meta. *Transparency Center: labeling AI content* (the "AI Info" label). https://transparency.meta.com/governance/tracking-impact/labeling-ai-content . **P** 18/11/2026. Read: Meta says it adds an "AI Info" label to video, audio and image content from May 2024; the label is applied when it detects industry-standard AI image indicators or when the uploader discloses AI generation; the page states the method is still evolving and may miss some AI-edited content, and sets no detailed disclosure rule for users.
23. TikTok. *Newsroom: new labels for disclosing AI-generated content* (announcement of 19/09/2023). https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/new-labels-for-disclosing-ai-generated-content . **P** 18/11/2026. Read: the synthetic media policy asks creators to label realistic AI-generated images, audio or video; a creator label applies to content completely generated or significantly edited by AI, and a caption or sticker is an alternative; TikTok said it would test an automatic label for detected AI content; no penalties and no provenance standard are described. The page is from 2023; the current help-centre text was not read (the support URL redirected to a page that returned only a title).
