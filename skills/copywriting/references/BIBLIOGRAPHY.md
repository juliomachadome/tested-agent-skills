# Bibliography: copywriting

Status: **V** = identity (title, publisher, year; authors as far as the catalogue shows) matched to the ISBN in OpenLibrary (`https://openlibrary.org/isbn/<isbn>.json`) this pass; **P** = official or primary page opened (or local official file) and the relevant text read this pass; for a paper, the DOI record matched title, author, venue and year (said where the publisher page was not opened); **S** = exists, text not read (e.g. needs a browser pass); **U** = not verified. Labels: BOOK, PRACTITIONER (single-voice teaching, no data), PAPER, GUIDE (style or UX guidance), OFFICIAL (law, regulation, code). Nothing is quoted; ideas are restated.
