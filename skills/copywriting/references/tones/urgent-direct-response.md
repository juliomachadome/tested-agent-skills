# Tone: urgent and direct-response

## When it fits
Info-products, courses, launches, sales with a real end date. Energetic, benefit-first, willing to ask for the sale. It works only when the reason to act soon is true.

## Word choices
- Direct verbs and "you": "Join before Friday 23:59 CET."
- A concrete benefit and a number in the first line; proof next to every promise.
- Real deadlines with date, time and time zone; real quantities (seats, cohort size) stated as facts.

## Sentence rhythm
Short, forward-moving. Open loops are fine if closed in the same page. Bullets for benefits; bold the fact that matters. Repeat the call to action after each proof block. Each paragraph earns the next.

## Do
- Make the deadline the real one and say what happens after it (price returns to 69; next cohort in May).
- Put price, terms, renewal and guarantee next to the button.
- Use customer proof that you have consent for and can verify.
- Offer a clear "not for you if..." line; it raises trust and cuts refunds.

## Avoid
- Countdown timers that reset, fake stock for digital goods, deadlines that quietly extend, "last chance" repeated for weeks.
- Income, weight-loss, health or guaranteed-result promises you cannot prove.
- Fake testimonials, invented numbers, hidden material connections, confirm-shaming declines.
- Fear, shame or exploitation of vulnerability.

## Before and after (same message: a course closing)
Before: "FINAL HOURS!!! Don't miss out or you will regret it forever! Only 2 spots left!!!"
After: "Enrolment closes Friday 14 March, 23:59 CET. Price EUR 49 now, EUR 69 from 15 March. 120 students in this cohort; 18 places left (live count). 14-day refund, no questions."

## Second example (same message: a launch e-mail)
Before: "LAST CHANCE!!! Everything disappears at midnight!!! Act NOW."
After: "Subject: The workshop closes tonight at 23:59 CET. Body: 40 seats, 6 left as of 14:00. After tonight the next intake is in June. Here is what you get, the price (EUR 99 incl. VAT) and the 14-day refund. Join here."

## Quick checks
- Is every deadline, price and quantity true and logged with its source?
- Does the deadline hold after it passes? If it will be extended, do not call it a deadline.
- Does the page show price, terms and refund path near the button?
- Is the refund rate in the test plan as a guardrail?

## Risks
- Legal EU: false limited-offer or scarcity claims and fake urgency are listed unfair practices (Directive 2005/29/EC, Annex I; verified via the Commission page, annex text needs a browser pass); price-reduction rules apply to "was/now" prices (Directive 2019/2161); withdrawal and payment-button obligations stay as written (`../eu-consumer-law.md`). US FTC endorsement guides (16 CFR 255): disclose material connections, endorsements must reflect honest experience. Brazil: CDC art. 37 (misleading and abusive advertising, incl. exploiting fear), art. 38 (proof burden on the sponsor); CONAR art. 1 and art. 27 section 9 (testimonials genuine and verifiable). Not legal advice.
- Accessibility: countdown widgets need text equivalents and no auto-updating that disturbs screen readers; avoid flashing.
- Translation: direct pressure reads as aggressive in some markets; adapt.
- Failure mode: high conversions, high refunds and complaints. Track refund rate as a guardrail.
