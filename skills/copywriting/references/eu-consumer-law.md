# EU law that limits copy (any sale to EU consumers)

Not legal advice; national law can go further. Official sources: Directive 2005/29/EC (UCPD) and Annex I; Directive 2011/83/EU (CRD); Directive 98/6/EC art. 6a ("Omnibus"); Commission Guidance 2021/C 526/01 (UCPD) and 2021/C 526/02 (art. 6a). Article numbers cited from memory: verify before a legal decision. All items **[C][EU]**. Price-reduction detail lives in the `offers` skill (`references/eu-consumer-law.md`).

- **False urgency and scarcity are banned in all circumstances** (Annex I no. 7): claiming a product or condition is available only for a very short time, untruthfully, to force an immediate decision. Resetting timers, "last spots" on digital products and extended deadlines fall here. In the Commission's 2022 sweep, 148 of 399 shops used at least one manipulative pattern and 42 used fake timers (published Jan 2023).
- **"Free" only for what costs nothing** beyond the unavoidable cost of responding and collecting (no. 20). An item always inside the bundle price is part of the product, not a gift. "Buy X, get Y" only if X was not inflated to pay for Y.
- **Reviews and testimonials:** banned to claim they come from real users without reasonable verification (no. 23b); banned to commission, invent or distort reviews (no. 23c); whoever shows reviews says whether and how they verify (UCPD art. 7(6)). Incentivised reviews must be labelled.
- **Legal rights are not offer features** (no. 10): the statutory withdrawal period is not "our guarantee"; a commercial guarantee is what you give beyond the law.
- **Posing as a consumer or hiding advertising is banned** (no. 22 and no. 11). A fictitious persona presented as a real person, customer or expert is misleading (arts. 6-7). Synthetic content imitating real people (voice, face) has transparency duties under the AI Act art. 50, applicable from 02/08/2026 (check the current text before publishing generated video or audio).
- **Outcome promises need proof.** "Fluent in 30 days", "guaranteed results": without evidence, a misleading action (art. 6). Describing what the person does and receives is always safe; promising what they achieve needs data.
- **Price reductions** (strike-through, "−20%", "sale", "Black Friday" implying a cut): show the lowest price of the prior 30 days and compute the advantage on it (art. 6a; CJEU C-330/23). At EU level art. 6a covers goods; services and digital content answer to the UCPD, and countries may extend the rule.
- **Dark patterns** fall under the UCPD even without their own Annex item: confirmshaming, nagging, pre-selected expensive option, exit harder than entry, essential information hidden (Guidance 2021/C 526/01, 4.2.7; taxonomy: deceptive.design; Mathur et al. 2019 found such patterns on 11.1% of 11k shops).
- **Pay-obligation button:** the final button states unambiguously that ordering means paying; otherwise the consumer is not bound (CRD art. 8(2)). Do not swap the label for a "creative" phrase.
- **Extra payments need express consent:** pre-ticked add-ons are banned and refundable (CRD art. 22).
- **Immediate-access digital content:** the 14-day withdrawal right is lost only with prior express consent, acknowledgement of the loss and confirmation on a durable medium (arts. 16(m), 8(7)). That checkbox text is legal text: copy does not rewrite or "soften" it. Since 19/06/2026 the CRD also foresees an online withdrawal function (art. 11a, Directive 2023/2673); transposition varies.
- **Marketing e-mail and SMS need prior consent** (Directive 2002/58 art. 13(1)); the soft opt-in for existing customers and similar products (art. 13(2)) depends on national transposition. The CJEU (C-654/23, 13/11/2025, secondary summaries) held that a newsletter promoting paid content is direct marketing and the exception is read strictly.
- **Personalised prices by automated decision must be disclosed** (CRD art. 6(1)(ea)).
- **Pending:** the Commission is preparing a Digital Fairness Act (dark patterns, addictive design, subscriptions, influencers). Check its status before long-term decisions.

## Legal checklist for every piece
- [ ] No deadline, stock or timer that is not real and final.
- [ ] "Free" only for what costs nothing; nothing in the price called a free gift.
- [ ] Any reduction signal carries the 30-day lowest price as national law requires.
- [ ] Legal rights not sold as advantages.
- [ ] No persona, voice or synthetic face presented as a real person.
- [ ] Reviews real, verified, verification method published.
