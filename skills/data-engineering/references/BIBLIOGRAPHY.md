## Books
1. Kimball, R., Ross, M. *The Data Warehouse Toolkit: The Definitive Guide to Dimensional Modeling*, 3rd ed. Wiley, 2013. ISBN 978-1118530801. **V** (the catalogue record lists Kimball; Ross is the co-author on the book's cover, not shown in that record). BOOK. Supports: grain, facts, dimensions, conformed dimensions, slowly changing dimension types (`modelling.md`).
2. Reis, J., Housley, M. *Fundamentals of Data Engineering*. O'Reilly, 2022. ISBN 978-1098108304. **V**. BOOK. Supports: lifecycle (generation, ingestion, storage, transformation, serving), undercurrents (security, data management, orchestration, DataOps), choosing tools by team size (`SKILL.md` 2, 8).
3. Kleppmann, M. *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*. O'Reilly, 2017. ISBN 978-1449373320. **V**. BOOK. Supports: at-least-once delivery and idempotence, change data capture, log-based pipelines, event time vs processing time (`ingestion.md`, `diagnosis-playbook.md` Tree C).
4. Linstedt, D., Olschimke, M. *Building a Scalable Data Warehouse with Data Vault 2.0*. Morgan Kaufmann, 2015. ISBN 978-0128025109. **V**. BOOK (written by the method's authors, who sell training). Supports: hubs, links, satellites, when the method pays (`modelling.md`).
5. Kimball, R., Ross, M., Thornthwaite, W. and others. *The Data Warehouse Lifecycle Toolkit*, 2nd ed. Wiley, 2011. ISBN 978-1118079560. **V** (authors per catalogue records; edition year 2011). BOOK. Supports: business-process-first modelling, bus matrix (`modelling.md`).
6. Inmon, W. H. *Building the Data Warehouse*, 4th ed. Wiley, 2005. ISBN 978-0764599446. **V**. BOOK. Supports: the normalised enterprise warehouse approach as the alternative to Kimball (`modelling.md`).
7. Kimball, R. (lead author) and co-author. *The Data Warehouse ETL Toolkit*. Wiley, 2004. ISBN 978-0764567575. **V** (catalogue shows Kimball only; co-author not confirmed here). BOOK, old tooling, durable ideas. Supports: staging, surrogate keys, auditing and error handling (`playbooks.md`).
8. Moses, B. and co-authors. *Data Quality Fundamentals*. O'Reilly, 2022. ISBN 978-1098112042. **V** (first author per catalogue; co-authors not resolved). PRACTITIONER (authors work at a data observability vendor). Supports: freshness, volume, schema and distribution checks (`quality.md`).
9. Winand, M. *SQL Performance Explained*. Self-published, 2012. ISBN 978-3950307825. **V**. BOOK. Supports: indexes in row stores, why a warehouse differs (`SKILL.md` 3).
10. Akidau, T. and co-authors. *Streaming Systems*. O'Reilly, 2018. ISBN 978-1491983874. **V** (first author per catalogue). BOOK. Supports: event time, watermarks, late data (`ingestion.md`).
11. Dehghani, Z. *Data Mesh*. O'Reilly, 2022. ISBN 978-1492092391. **V**. BOOK, one organisational thesis; relevant to large companies, not small teams. Supports: data products and ownership, with that limit (`quality.md`).

## Official documentation
12. dbt, *Incremental models*. https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/build/incremental-models . **P** 18/11/2026: strategies (merge, delete+insert, insert_overwrite, microbatch), `unique_key` defines grain and must be unique, `on_schema_change` options (ignore is default), `--full-refresh`, `is_incremental()` filter with `>=`. The page has no section on late data; the lookback advice here is **[F]** inference.
13. dbt, *Snapshots*. https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/build/snapshots . **P** 18/11/2026: timestamp and check strategies, `dbt_valid_from` and `dbt_valid_to`, `hard_deletes`, YAML config from v1.9, snapshots cannot be rebuilt so keep them in a restricted schema.
14. dbt, *Data tests*. https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/build/data-tests . **P** 18/11/2026: four generic tests, singular tests, `severity`, `warn_if`/`error_if`, `store_failures`, rename from "tests" to "data tests".
15. dbt, *Freshness*. https://docs.getdbt.com/reference/resource-properties/freshness . **P** 18/11/2026: `loaded_at_field`, `warn_after`, `error_after`, `dbt freshness`, `filter`.
16. dbt, *Model contracts*. https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/mesh/govern/model-contracts . **P** 18/11/2026: enforced column names and types, constraints, enforcement varies by platform, works for table and incremental, not views' constraints, breaking changes call for a new version.
17. Apache Airflow, *Dags*. https://airflow.apache.org/docs/apache-airflow/stable/core-concepts/dags.html . **P** 18/11/2026: data interval, logical date, backfill by running past periods, retries via `default_args`. The page gives no idempotency guidance; that is **[F]** here.
18. Dagster, *Concepts*. https://dagster.io/docs/getting-started/concepts . **P** 18/11/2026 (docs.dagster.io redirects here): assets, jobs, schedules, sensors, partitions. Backfills not described on that page.
19. Google Cloud, *Introduction to partitioned tables* (BigQuery). https://docs.cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs/partitioned-tables . **P** 18/11/2026: integer-range, time-unit column and ingestion-time partitioning, pruning on the partition column, dry run estimate, clustering vs partitioning. The page does not state the partition limit; check the quotas page.
20. Google Cloud, *Control costs in BigQuery*. https://docs.cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs/best-practices-costs . **P** 18/11/2026: dry run as an upper bound, maximum bytes billed, LIMIT does not cut bytes on non-clustered tables, staged materialisation, on-demand vs capacity pricing. Prices are not read; always check the current price page.
21. Snowflake, *Clustering keys and clustered tables*. https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/tables-clustering-keys . **P** 18/11/2026: micro-partitions, clustering for very large tables with selective queries, reclustering costs credits and storage, 3-4 columns, cardinality advice.
22. PostgreSQL, *INSERT* (current docs). https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/sql-insert.html . **P** 18/11/2026: `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` / `DO UPDATE`, conflict target rules, `EXCLUDED`, one command cannot update the same row twice, `RETURNING` semantics.
23. PostgreSQL, *Logical decoding concepts*. https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/logicaldecoding-explanation.html . **P** 18/11/2026: logical decoding from WAL, replication slots persist and retain WAL when unused, output plugins.
24. PostgreSQL, *Date/time types*. https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/datatype-datetime.html . **P** 18/11/2026: `timestamptz` stored as UTC and displayed in session TimeZone; `timestamp` ignores zones.
25. OpenLineage, *Documentation home*. https://openlineage.io/docs/ . **P** 18/11/2026: open lineage specification; jobs, runs, datasets, facets.

28. dbt, *State comparison caveats*. https://docs.getdbt.com/reference/node-selection/state-comparison-caveats . **P** 18/11/2026: `state:modified` compares code and config not data, tags and meta ignored, `--state` vs target path, scheduled jobs may build nothing, relationships tests across environments. The full `--defer` definition is on another page not read; the exact slim CI command in `advanced-topics.md` 4 is **[F]**.

30. Feast, *Point-in-time joins*. https://docs.feast.dev/getting-started/concepts/point-in-time-joins . **P** 18/11/2026: feature state as of each row's timestamp, leakage from late-written values, created-timestamp filter, TTL measured from the entity timestamp (DOCS, one feature store).
