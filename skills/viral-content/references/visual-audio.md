# Visual and audio levers

Script decides what is said; image and sound decide whether anyone stays to hear it. Each lever below names the mechanism from `SKILL.md` section 2, how to test it (one change at a time, medians over 2-3 posts) and the limits. Evidence tags as in the skill; most items are practitioner rules **[F]** unless a bibliography number is given. Specs (resolution, safe zones, export, loudness targets) live in `../../ad-creatives/references/specs-safe-zones-audio.md`; do not duplicate them here.

## Image
| Lever | Mechanism | Do | Test by | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First frame | Stop: the scroll decision is made before any word lands | The premise visible in frame 1 (the result, the impossible situation, the number); one subject, high contrast, readable at thumb size | Same video, two first frames | A misleading first frame wins the stop and loses the hold |
| Faces and eyes | Stop and stay: people look at faces and follow gaze **[F]** | A face in frame 1 when the story has a protagonist; gaze pointing at the key object or text | Face vs no face opening | Stock or uncanny AI faces lower trust |
| Motion onset | Stop: new motion in the periphery captures attention **[F]** | Something starts moving in the first second (a counter, a zoom, a hand) | Static vs moving first second | Constant motion without change tires the eye |
| On-screen numbers (HUD) | Stay: concrete numbers create small questions and a game feel (curiosity, bib. 16) | Counters, timers, percentages that change with the story; each one answers or raises a question | HUD on vs off on the same episode | Numbers that mean nothing become noise |
| Visual consistency | Follow and return: a recognisable look and character make the series findable and a parasocial bond easier (bib. on parasocial ties) | Same character design, palette, type and layout every episode; consistent AI character sheets and seeds | Retention of part N among returning viewers | Consistency is not sameness: change the scene, keep the identity |
| Cut rate | Stay: each cut resets attention, but too many cuts lose the thread | Cut when information changes, not on a timer; the field study shows 2-5 cuts per 10 s on narrated stories (`field-study-10-reels.md`) | Retention curve around dense vs calm sections | Copying a cut rate without new information per cut does not work |
| Captions | Stay with sound off; processing fluency | Word-by-word burned-in captions, large, in the safe zone, current word highlighted | Captions vs none | Walls of text slow reading; never hide the subject |

## Sound
| Lever | Mechanism | Do | Test by | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Voice | Finish: a continuous, confident voice carries narrative transportation (bib. 18) | One narrator, present tense, short sentences, pauses before reveals; consistent voice across episodes | Two reads of the same script | AI voices: disclose where the platform or law requires it; never clone a real person without written consent |
| Sound effects on events | Stay: audio marks a change and resets attention **[F]** | A short effect on each reveal, number change or cut that matters | With vs without effects | Effects on every cut become wallpaper |
| Music | Emotion and pace (arousal, bib. 5-6 as a hypothesis) | Music under the voice, ducked so speech stays clear; tension builds to the decision point | Same edit, two beds | Use licensed or platform-library tracks; commercial accounts often cannot use trending songs |
| Loudness and clarity | Stay: listeners skip audio that is hard to hear | Speech clear over music, levels consistent between episodes (targets in the ad-creatives specs file) | Listen on a phone speaker before posting | Platform normalisation changes perceived level; check on device |
| Sound off | Most feeds start muted for many viewers **[F]** | The story must make sense from image and captions alone | Watch the export muted | Never rely on a spoken line for the hook only |

## Diagnosis add-ons
- Drop in the first 1-2 s with a good hook line: the first frame or the first second of motion is weak; fix image before script.
- Drop at the same second in several videos: look at what the screen and the sound do there (a long static shot, music dropping out, a caption block).
- High views, low follows on a series: visual identity changes too much between parts, or nothing shows it is part N of a series (part tag, consistent title card).

## Rights and labels
Music: library or licensed tracks only (`playbooks.md` trends and rights). AI images and voices: label realistic AI content as each platform asks; keep consent for any real voice or face; do not imitate a living person's voice or likeness.
