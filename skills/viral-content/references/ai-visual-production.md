## Levers that matter for reach
- **Consistency is a viewer lever**: a repeating look, character and voice is processed faster and makes a series recognisable (bib. 23-25 **[M]**). Lock a style and a character before episode 1; change them only between seasons. Mechanics: `media-production`.
- **First frame**: the most expressive image of the piece, one clear subject, readable on mute in 1-2 s (`visual-audio.md`).
- **Vertical framing**: subject and eyes in the top two-thirds, calm lower third for captions and platform UI; safe zones in `../../ad-creatives/references/specs-safe-zones-audio.md`.
- **Variety inside the look**: change shot size between cuts so the series feels edited, not like a slideshow **[F]**.
- **Real text layers**: captions, counters and signs are never generated pixels.
- **Labels and rights**: realistic AI content needs the platform label and, in the EU, art. 50 disclosure; no real person's likeness or voice without consent; music for business accounts from the commercial library or a licence. Rules and sources: `media-production` (`rights-and-labels.md`); check the country and platform.
- **Cost keeps the series alive**: a series that cannot be made weekly stops. Estimate cost and time per episode in `media-production` before committing to a cadence.
