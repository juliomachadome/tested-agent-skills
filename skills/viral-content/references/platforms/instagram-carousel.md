# Instagram carousel

Tags and bib. numbers as in `../../SKILL.md` and `../BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`. **[F]** = not confirmed on a page read.

## How distribution works
- A carousel is a feed post, so Feed ranking applies: your activity (likes, shares, saves, comments), how popular the post is, how much the poster engages, your history with the poster, and recency (29, re-read 18/11/2026).
- Feed predictions named: time spent on the post, commenting, liking, sharing, tapping the profile photo (29). Time spent favours posts people swipe through.
- Explore weights post popularity more and predicts likes, saves and shares (29). Recommendation eligibility follows the same guidelines as other formats (52).
- Instagram can resurface a carousel by showing a later slide to someone who skipped it **[F]**.

## Metrics that matter
Reach, shares, saves, comments, profile activity; for carousels, per-slide exits and forward or back taps appear under the navigation metrics (names seen on the Insights help page, depth not read; check the app **[F]**). Saves and shares are the utility signal. Compare followers vs non-followers.

## Format and length
- Several slides in one post; the maximum slide count and ratios (1:1, 4:5 and 3:4 are used) change by release **[F]**: mixing ratios crops, so keep one ratio.
- Slide 1 acts as the cover: big subject, one promise, readable at thumbnail size. Keep key text inside the centre 4:5 area, since the grid view crops **[F]**; see `../../../ad-creatives/references/specs-safe-zones-audio.md`.
- Real text layers, high contrast, one idea per slide.

## Hook and structure patterns that fit
- Slide 1 hook, slides 2-n one step each, last slide a recap plus one next step (save, send, follow).
- Utility structures: checklist, step-by-step, before/after, myth vs fact (`../playbooks.md`).
- Story-led carousels: each slide ends with a reason to swipe.
- Slide 2 should deliver value fast so the swipe is rewarded.

## Cadence [F]
2-4 a week is a common test range; no source read gives a number.

## What fails or gets suppressed
Reposted or watermarked graphics and low-quality files are listed as not eligible for recommendation (52); text-heavy low-resolution slides; a cover that promises more than the slides deliver (fewer saves **[F]**); undisclosed paid partnerships (33).

## Checklist
1. Cover states one promise at thumbnail size.
2. One idea per slide, one aspect ratio.
3. Own artwork or photos, no watermarks.
4. Last slide gives a save or send reason and a next step.
5. Judge by saves and shares per reach, median of 10.
