# TikTok (For You feed)

Tags as in `../../SKILL.md`: **[F]** platform or practitioner statement, unverified beyond the page named. Bib. numbers refer to `../BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`.

## How distribution works
- Each viewer gets a personal feed, ranked by the predicted chance that this viewer is interested (30, re-read 18/11/2026).
- Signals: likes, shares, follows, comments, content the viewer creates, "not interested", hiding a creator or sound; content info (captions, sounds, hashtags); language, country, device, which count least (30).
- Stronger signals weigh more: finishing a long video is strong, sharing a country with the creator is weak (30).
- Follower count and past hits are not direct ranking factors, so a small account can reach strangers (30).
- Not recommended: new or under-review uploads until cleared, spam meant to inflate traffic, duplicate content, videos already seen. The feed avoids back-to-back videos with the same sound or creator and mixes in some off-topic ones to help discovery (30).

## Metrics that matter
Views; average watch time and "watched full video" rate; shares; saves; comments; follows from the video; traffic source (For You vs profile vs search). Where: Analytics in the creator tools, per video; names and menu paths change, so open the app and check **[F]** (the TikTok support page tried returned headings only, not used). Judge by medians over 10+ posts (`../diagnosis-playbook.md`).

## Format and length
- Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920 is the common target **[F]**. Lengths run from seconds to many minutes; the platform says finishing a longer video is a strong signal (30), so length is a choice: the shortest cut that keeps the promise, not a target number.
- Keep captions, buttons and the sound label in mind: leave the bottom ~20% and the right edge free of key text **[F]**; confirm in `../../../ad-creatives/references/specs-safe-zones-audio.md`.
- Burned-in captions: many watch muted, many with sound; design for both **[F]**.

## Hook and structure patterns that fit
- A sound-on, person-led, fast-paced hook; native, imperfect, lo-fi usually reads as content, not ads **[F]**.
- Hook in the first 1-2 s: unexpected visual or a direct claim of stakes (`../playbooks.md` section 1); pay it off before the end so completion and rewatch rise.
- Loop endings: last frame leads back to the first, lifts replays **[F]**.
- Series and reply-to-comment videos build the follow reason.
- Trending sounds help only if they fit the mechanism; business accounts need the commercial library (`../playbooks.md` section 4).

## Cadence [F]
Practitioners report 3-7 posts a week for growth tests and at least 1-2 for upkeep; no platform source read gives a number. Test one cadence per batch.

## What fails or gets suppressed
Spam or engagement baiting; reposted or duplicated videos (30); other apps' watermarks **[F]**; videos still under review; unlabelled realistic AI content (label rules: check the current TikTok page, the one tried did not render; EU art. 50 in 35, 51). Banned or restricted topics reduce eligibility (30).

## Checklist
1. One viewer, one promise, shown in the first 2 s.
2. Original file, no foreign watermark, not a repeat.
3. Text and faces clear of caption and button zones.
4. Payoff delivered; ending invites share, save or the next part.
5. Metric to judge: watch-through rate and shares, median of 10 posts.
