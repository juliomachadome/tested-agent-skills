# YouTube long-form

Tags and bib. numbers as in `../../SKILL.md` and `../BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`. **[F]** = not confirmed on a page read.

## How distribution works
- Recommendations (Home, Up Next, Watch next) aim at what each viewer wants and values, using viewing behaviour, likes and dislikes, subscriptions, search and watch history, satisfaction surveys, channel reputation and quality, and external raters (31, re-read 18/11/2026).
- For news, finance and health topics the system promotes higher-quality sources (31).
- Two gates work in sequence: the viewer must click (title and thumbnail), then stay **[F]**; that is why the two metrics below are read together.

## Metrics that matter
- Reach: impressions, impressions click-through rate (how often viewers watched after seeing a thumbnail; formula not given on the page), views, unique viewers, traffic sources. Where: Studio > Content > video > Insights > Reach (54).
- Engagement: watch time, average view duration (minutes among those who stayed), retention graph with "Intro" (share still watching after 30 s), "Top moments", and "Typical retention" against your 10 latest similar-length videos; data after 1-2 days (53).
- A flat retention line means they stay, gradual decline means fading interest, spikes mean rewatch or sharing, dips mean skipping (53).

## Format and length
- Standard 16:9 on computer; vertical and square play too (56). Upload as MP4, H.264, AAC audio; 1080p at about 8 Mbps, 4K 35-45 Mbps (56).
- Thumbnail: 16:9, 3840x2160 recommended, minimum width 640 px, JPG or PNG, 2 MB limit on mobile and 50 MB on desktop (55).
- Upload length limits were not on the page read.

## Hook and structure patterns that fit
- Package first: a title and thumbnail that promise one specific payoff, honest to the video (`../playbooks.md` section 1 for hook types).
- First 30 s: confirm the promise, show the stakes, skip long intros (the Intro metric exists for this).
- Re-hooks at retention dips: open a new loop mid-video when the graph falls **[F]**.
- Chapters and a next-video pointer at the end (end screens) raise session value **[F]**.
- Cut a Short from a strong moment and point to the long video.

## Cadence [F]
Usually weekly to a few per month; quality and a consistent topic beat volume per channel reputation signals (31). No numeric source read.

## What fails or gets suppressed
Clickbait the video does not pay off (low retention, lower recommendation **[F]**); reused or mass-produced content, inauthentic content (policy page not opened here **[F]**); undisclosed realistic synthetic content (48).

## Checklist
1. One promise in title, thumbnail and first 30 s.
2. Thumbnail readable at phone size, 16:9 file within limits.
3. Retention graph reviewed; fix the biggest dip next time.
4. Chapters, description, end screen to the next video.
5. Judge by CTR and average view duration together, over 5+ videos.
6. Conversion: long videos convert best; one clear next step (link in description and pinned comment, end screen), measured as clicks and sales per 1,000 views (`../virality-to-conversion.md`).
