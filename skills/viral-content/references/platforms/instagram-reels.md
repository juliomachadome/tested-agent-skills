# Instagram Reels

Tags and bib. numbers as in `../../SKILL.md` and `../BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`. **[F]** = not confirmed on a page read.

## How distribution works
- Instagram says each surface ranks differently. For Reels the signals are your activity, your history with the poster, the reel's content (audio and visuals), and the poster's popularity (followers and engagement) (29, re-read 18/11/2026).
- Predictions named: chance of resharing, watching to the end, liking, and visiting the audio page (29).
- Reels aimed at non-followers go to recommendation surfaces, which require the Recommendation Guidelines; unoriginal content, watermarks, reposts and low quality are listed as not eligible (52). Repeated violations can make posts ineligible for a period; Account Status shows eligibility (29).

## Metrics that matter
Reach, plays, watch time, shares, saves, profile activity (insights menu names these for reels, stories, posts and carousels; exact paths not read, check in the app **[F]**). Priority order by the stated predictions: reshares (sends), average watch and completion, likes, audio-page visits. Compare followers vs non-followers reach. Median of 10+ posts (`../diagnosis-playbook.md`).

## Format and length
- Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920 **[F]**. Length limits change by release; choose by story, not the maximum **[F]**.
- Low resolution, borders, muted audio, mostly text, watermarks and previously posted content may get less distribution to non-followers (29). Export clean, with sound, full-bleed.
- Safe zones: the lower part and right edge carry caption and buttons; the profile grid crops to a centre rectangle, so set the cover **[F]**; see `../../../ad-creatives/references/specs-safe-zones-audio.md`.

## Hook and structure patterns that fit
- Sendable by design: the viewer must be able to name a person to send it to (`../SKILL.md` ladder, share).
- 1-2 s visual hook, captions on screen, payoff before the end so completion holds.
- Audio: original sound or licensed track; the audio-page visit is a ranking prediction, so a distinctive original sound can create its own page **[F]**.
- Series label plus a stated next part for follows.

## Cadence [F]
Common advice is 3-5 reels a week; no Instagram page read gives a number. Test one cadence per batch.

## What fails or gets suppressed
Watermarked TikTok exports and reposts (29, 52); low-resolution or bordered files (29); unlabelled realistic AI content (Instagram's own label page did not load here; check the current policy, and EU art. 50 in 35, 51); branded content without the partner tag (33).

## Checklist
1. Clean 9:16 file, no foreign watermark, with sound.
2. Hook in 2 s, payoff before the end.
3. A named person it would be sent to.
4. Text clear of the bottom and right UI zones.
5. Judge by sends per reach and completion over 10 posts.
