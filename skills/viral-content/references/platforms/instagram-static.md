# Instagram static feed post (single image)

Tags and bib. numbers as in `../../SKILL.md` and `../BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`. **[F]** = not confirmed on a page read.

## How distribution works
- Feed ranking uses your activity, post popularity and recency, poster engagement and your history with the poster (29, re-read 18/11/2026).
- Predictions named: time spent on the post, comment, like, share, tap on the profile photo (29). A single image earns time spent through a strong, quickly readable idea or a long caption people read.
- Explore weights popularity more and predicts likes, saves and shares (29). Eligibility needs the Recommendation Guidelines (52).
- Reach to non-followers is generally lower than for Reels **[F]**; a static post mostly serves existing followers and profile credibility.

## Metrics that matter
Reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, profile activity (names from the Insights help page, menu paths not read, check in the app **[F]**). Saves and shares signal value; profile visits and follows show the pull to the account. Compare to the median of your last 10 static posts, not to Reels.

## Format and length
- Common ratios 1:1, 4:5 and 3:4; the profile grid crops to a centre rectangle (shape depends on release) **[F]**; keep the subject centred.
- High resolution, no borders, no watermark (watermarks and low quality are listed as not eligible for recommendation, 52).
- Alt text and a keyword-rich first caption line help accessibility and search **[F]**.
- Captions: the first lines show before "more"; put the hook there **[F]**.

## Hook and structure patterns that fit
- One idea, one image: quote card, chart, before/after, product in use, a person with a clear expression.
- A caption that asks one concrete question or gives a save reason.
- Photographic proof beats stock; faces draw attention (bib. 20, lab study **[M]**).
- Use it for announcements, proof and brand consistency; use Reels for reach.

## Cadence [F]
2-5 per week is a common range alongside other formats; no source read gives a number.

## What fails or gets suppressed
Reposts, watermarked or low-quality images (52); engagement-bait captions **[F]**; hidden paid partnerships without the tag (33); realistic AI imagery without disclosure where required (35, 51).

## Checklist
1. One idea readable in 1 s at phone size.
2. Subject centred, 4:5 safe for the grid.
3. Original image, no watermark.
4. Hook in the caption's first line plus one next step.
5. Judge by saves, shares and profile visits per reach.
