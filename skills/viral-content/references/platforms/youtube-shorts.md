# YouTube Shorts

Tags and bib. numbers as in `../../SKILL.md` and `../BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`. **[F]** = platform or practitioner statement not confirmed on a page read.

## How distribution works
- Viewers find Shorts in the Shorts tab (a continuous feed) and also in search, the home page, channel pages, Subscriptions and notifications (32, re-read 18/11/2026).
- YouTube's recommendation page lists viewing behaviour, likes and dislikes, subscriptions, search and watch history, satisfaction surveys, channel reputation and quality, and external raters as inputs (31, re-read). That page does not describe the Shorts feed specifically, so "how the Shorts feed ranks" beyond those inputs is **[F]**.

## Metrics that matter
- **Views** count every start or replay with no minimum watch time since 31 Mar 2025; **Engaged views** keeps the old measure and still drives Partner Programme eligibility and Shorts revenue share (32). Look at engaged views and the ratio to views.
- Retention graph per video: YouTube Studio > Content > the video > Analytics > Engagement > Audience retention; "Typical retention" compares with your last 10 similar videos, "Top moments" shows where nobody left; data takes 1-2 days (53).
- Traffic sources, including the Shorts feed: Studio > Content > video > Insights > Reach (54). Also subscribers gained per Short **[F]**.

## Format and length
- Up to 3 minutes, vertical, up to 1080p (32). Aspect ratio exact figure not given on that page; vertical 9:16 is the usual **[F]**.
- Custom Shorts thumbnail: 9:16, 2160x3840 recommended, minimum height 640 px, JPG or PNG, up to 50 MB on desktop (55). Many viewers never see it in the feed, so the first frame matters more **[F]**.
- Safe zones: keep text out of the bottom caption and right button area **[F]**; see `../../../ad-creatives/references/specs-safe-zones-audio.md`.

## Hook and structure patterns that fit
- Answer the question in the first 3 s or promise it clearly; viewers swipe fast.
- Loop-able structure: because every replay counts as a view, a tidy loop raises views but check engaged views, not only views.
- A Short can lead to a long video on the same topic (series playlists) **[F]**.
- Reuse a TikTok or Reel only if it is your own file, edited for this surface.

## Cadence [F]
Channels commonly test daily to 3 a week; the page read gives no number. Test one cadence per batch.

## What fails or gets suppressed
Reposts of others' clips and unoriginal compilations (reuse rules sit in YouTube's monetisation policies, not opened here **[F]**); realistic altered or synthetic content must be disclosed, not needed for obviously unrealistic content or minor edits (48); low channel quality lowers distribution (31, "channel reputation and quality").

## Checklist
1. First frame readable on mute, one clear idea.
2. Under 3 min, vertical, own footage.
3. Loop or a hard payoff; no filler middle.
4. Disclosure toggle set if realistic AI.
5. Read engaged views and retention, not only views.
6. Conversion: views rarely sell by themselves; pin a comment or related video to the next step, and judge by follows and clicks per 1,000 views, not views (`../virality-to-conversion.md`).
