# Instagram Stories

Tags and bib. numbers as in `../../SKILL.md` and `../BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`. **[F]** = not confirmed on a page read.

## How distribution works
- Stories are ranked for people who already follow you: signals are viewing history, engagement history (likes, DMs) and closeness to the author (29, re-read 18/11/2026).
- Predictions named: whether the viewer will tap into a story, reply, or skip to the next (29).
- No Stories-specific creator advice is given on that page (29). Close Friends is a viewer-side control to limit an audience (29).
- Stories are mainly a retention and relationship surface, not a stranger-reach one; to reach new people use Reels or carousels **[F]**.

## Metrics that matter
Reach, forward taps, back taps, exits, replies, shares, sticker taps, profile visits and link taps (the Insights help page names reach, navigation forward, back and exits, shares and profile activity; sticker and link details not read, check the app **[F]**). Exits and next-story taps show where people lose interest.

## Format and length
- Full-screen vertical 9:16, 1080x1920 **[F]**; each segment is short (about 60 s for video, 5-7 s for a still) **[F]**.
- Safe zones: keep key text away from the top profile bar and the bottom reply bar, about 14% at each edge **[F]**; see `../../../ad-creatives/references/specs-safe-zones-audio.md`.
- Stickers (poll, question, link) are interaction tools; each tap is a signal **[F]**.

## Hook and structure patterns that fit
- A sequence of 3-7 frames: hook frame, context, proof or step, one prompt to reply or tap.
- Behind-the-scenes, polls and question boxes build the "closeness" signal named above (29).
- A direct next step (DM keyword, link sticker) for conversion (`../virality-to-conversion.md`).
- Repeat showing up at a consistent time so followers learn to tap.

## Cadence [F]
Several frames most days is common practice; no source read gives a number.

## What fails or gets suppressed
Too many frames without a payoff (skips lower your next-story distribution, inferred from the stated skip prediction, **[F]**); blurry or text-only walls; undisclosed branded content (33); reposted material without permission.

## Checklist
1. First frame earns the tap.
2. 3-7 frames, one point per frame.
3. Text inside safe margins.
4. One interactive element or one next step.
5. Judge by completion (last-frame reach vs first) and replies.
