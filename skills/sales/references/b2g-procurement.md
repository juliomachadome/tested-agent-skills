# B2G: public procurement

**Not legal advice.** Procurement law differs by country, by level of government and by year, and it changes (thresholds are indexed, regulations are rewritten). The notice (edital, tender documents, solicitation) and the contracting authority's official channel always prevail over this page. For anything with legal consequences (eligibility, challenge deadlines, sanctions, a conflict of interest) ask a procurement lawyer.

**Verification status (checked 15/11/2026).** EU Directive 2014/24/EU: official EUR-Lex text opened and read; article numbers and thresholds below are updated from Delegated Regulation 2025/2152. US FAR: acquisition.gov pages for subparts 3.1, 6.1, 13.2 and 15.2 opened and read; figures that change (thresholds) not re-checked. Brazil Lei 14.133/2021: read on normas.leg.br (original publication text, https://normas.leg.br/?urn=urn:lex:br:federal:lei:2021-04-01;14133 ; planalto.gov.br was unreachable). The amounts below are the **original 2021 figures**: art. 182 has the federal Executive update the law's values every 1 January by IPCA-E (published on the PNCP), so current amounts must be taken from the latest decree, not from this page. Later amendments to the law were not read.

## 1. How a tender works (generic spine)
Need defined by the buyer, market check, notice published, clarification window, bids submitted by a deadline, formal evaluation against published criteria, award, standstill or challenge window, contract, delivery, payment. The buyer may not change the rules during the run; the vendor may not change the bid after the deadline except where the notice allows (price after live bidding, correction of formal errors). Rules decide, not rapport.

## 2. Brazil, Lei 14.133/2021 (new procurement law) [P]
- **Modalidades (arts 28, 6, 29):** pregão, concorrência, concurso, leilão, diálogo competitivo; no other modality may be created or combined (art. 28 par. 2). Pregão is mandatory for common goods and services, judged by lowest price or highest discount (art. 6 XLI); it does not apply to predominantly intellectual specialised services or to engineering works and services, except common engineering services listed in art. 6 XXI a (art. 29). Concorrência covers special goods and services and engineering works and services (art. 6 XXXVIII); concurso picks a technical, scientific or artistic work; leilão sells assets; diálogo competitivo is a dialogue with pre-selected bidders before final proposals (art. 6 XLII). The older Lei 8.666/93, Lei 10.520/2002 and RDC arts 1-47-A were revoked two years after publication (art. 193 II); contracts run under the rules they were tendered under.
- **Direct contracting:** inexigibilidade (art. 74) when competition is unviable: exclusive supplier, renowned artists, specialised intellectual services of notable specialisation (advertising excluded), things contracted by credenciamento, a property that must be that one; the authority must prove the unviability (par. 1). Dispensa (art. 75): at the original text, below R$ 100,000 for engineering works and services or vehicle maintenance and below R$ 50,000 for other services and purchases (updated yearly, see above), plus emergencies and calamity (VIII, contracts capped at 1 year, no extension) and other listed cases. A vendor can propose but the authority decides. Illegal direct contracting is a crime (Código Penal art. 337-E, 4 to 8 years).
- **Ata de registro de preços (SRP):** a procedure auxiliar (art. 78 IV); registered prices create a supply commitment for the vendor but do not oblige the administration to buy (art. 83); the ata lasts 1 year, extendable once for an equal period if the price stays advantageous (art. 84); non-participating bodies may join with limits (art. 86: each body up to 50% of registered quantities, all joiners together up to double, art. 86 pars 4-5). Plan stock, margin and indexation for orders that may never come.
- **Phases, in sequence (art. 17):** preparatory, publication of the edital, proposals and bids, judgement, qualification (habilitação), appeals, homologation. Qualification may come before bids and judgement if the edital says so and reasons are given (art. 17 par. 1). Tenders are preferably electronic, in-person only with justification and recorded in audio and video (par. 2). The pregoeiro is the agent who conducts a pregão (art. 8 par. 5).
- **Modo de disputa (art. 56):** open (public successive bids), closed (sealed until a set time), or both; closed alone is barred for lowest price or highest discount; open is barred for technique-and-price; after the best bid, if the gap to second place is at least 5%, the authority may restart open bidding for the other places (par. 4). Judgement criteria (art. 33): lowest price, highest discount, best technique or artistic content, technique and price, highest bid (leilão), highest economic return.
- **Minimum time to submit proposals, counted from publication of the edital (art. 55), in working days:** goods 8 (lowest price or highest discount) or 15 (other criteria); common services and common engineering works and services 10; special services and engineering 25; integrated contracting 60; semi-integrated 35; highest bid 15; technique and price or best technique 35. A relevant edital change means republication and restarting the periods unless it does not affect the proposals (par. 1).
- **Esclarecimento and impugnação (art. 164):** anyone may ask for clarification or challenge the edital up to **3 working days before opening**; the answer is due within 3 working days and no later than the last working day before opening. **Appeals (art. 165):** 3 working days from notice or the minutes, against proposal judgement, qualification or disqualification, annulment or revocation, unilateral termination; intention to appeal against judgement or qualification must be declared immediately or it is lost (par. 1 I); the authority has 3 working days to reconsider, then the higher authority decides within 10 working days (par. 2). Appeal against sanctions of warning, fine or impediment: 15 working days (art. 166). Working days count only days with office hours at the body (art. 183).
- **Disqualification of proposals (art. 59):** incurable defects, non-compliance with the specification, unworkable ("inexequíveis") prices or prices above the estimate, non-compliance with other edital demands. For engineering, below 75% of the budgeted value is deemed unworkable (par. 4). Tie-breaks (art. 60): final dispute, past contract performance, gender-equity actions, integrity programme.
- **Qualification evidence (art. 67):** technical attestations are limited to the parts of greatest relevance (at least 4% of the estimated value); minimum quantities may be demanded up to 50% of those parts; limits of time or place are barred (pars 1-2). A demand above that is a ground to challenge.
- **Portal and transparency:** the full edital and annexes must be published and kept on the PNCP (art. 54); preparatory documents not in the edital go there after homologation (par. 3). Reference prices use PNCP panels among other sources (art. 23).
- **Preferences:** microenterprises and small firms get the LC 123/2006 arts 42-49 advantages (art. 4), except for items above the small-firm revenue ceiling and for firms that already used the benefit up to the limit (art. 4 pars 1-2); check whether the vendor qualifies and whether it is a real status. Read 15/11/2026 on normas.leg.br (consolidated): art. 42 fiscal and labour regularity proof is needed only at contract signature; art. 43 documents are still submitted, with 5 working days (extendable) to regularise after being declared winner; art. 44 tie when the small firm is within 10% (5% in pregão); art. 48 exclusive tender for items up to R$ 80,000, subcontracting may be required, quota up to 25% for divisible goods, local priority up to 10%; art. 49 exceptions. **Lei Complementar 238 of 21/10/2026 changes arts 48 and 49 from 1 Jan 2027**: the R$ 80,000 ceiling is replaced by the Lei 14.133 art. 75 I value (updated under art. 182) and an optional MEI-only tender (up to half of the art. 75 II value) is added. Check which text applies at the tender date.
- **Contract term and changes:** term set by the edital (art. 105); continuous services and supplies up to 5 years (art. 106), extendable up to 10 years in total if the edital allows (art. 107); the authority may terminate without cost when credit is lacking (art. 106 III, with timing limits in par. 1). Unilateral changes: the contractor must accept increases or reductions up to 25% of the updated initial value (50% for building or equipment renovations) (art. 125). A price-adjustment index is mandatory in the edital whatever the term (art. 25 par. 7). Performance guarantee, if required, up to 5% of the initial value, up to 10% when justified (art. 98); contractor chooses among cash or bonds, surety insurance or bank guarantee (art. 96). Payments follow chronological order by funding source and category (art. 141).
- **Sanctions (arts 155-156):** warning (only for partial non-performance), fine of 0.5% to 30% of the contract value, impediment to bid and contract for up to 3 years with the body's own government level, and declaration of unsuitability for 3 to 6 years across all levels (arts 156 pars 3-5). Appeal within 15 working days (art. 166). Bid crimes sit in the Penal Code arts 337-E to 337-P, inserted by art. 178 of the law, with a minimum fine of 2% of contract value (337-P). Lei 12.846/2013 acts are tried together with the administrative infractions (art. 159), but the content of Lei 12.846 itself was not read.
- **Pricing items to expect:** estimated reference price (art. 23), unworkable-price rule (art. 59), a negotiation with the first-placed bidder after judgement (art. 61), indexation, performance guarantees, penalties; exact edital values and the pregão procedure steps (regulations and decrees) were not read, **[unverified]**.

## 3. EU, Directive 2014/24/EU (read from EUR-Lex)
- **Principles (art. 18):** equal treatment, non-discrimination, transparency, proportionality; design may not artificially narrow competition. Art. 24 and the conflict-of-interest article require authorities to prevent, identify and remedy conflicts.
- **Procedures:** open procedure (any operator may bid; minimum 35 days from notice dispatch, art. 27), restricted procedure (request to participate, minimum 30 days; then invited bidders), competitive procedure with negotiation, competitive dialogue, innovation partnership; negotiated without prior publication only in narrow cases. Frameworks (art. 33) may not exceed 4 years save justified cases; contracts under them follow the framework terms.
- **Thresholds (art. 4, net of VAT; Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2152 of 22/10/2025, applying 1 Jan 2026 for 2026-2027, read 15/11/2026):** works EUR 5,404,000; supplies and services of central government authorities EUR 140,000; other contracting authorities EUR 216,000 (the original 2014 figures were 5,186,000, 134,000 and 207,000, later 5,538,000, 143,000 and 221,000). They are revised every two years; check the next regulation from 2028. Below thresholds, national law (and Treaty principles for cross-border interest) applies.
- **Award (art. 67):** most economically advantageous tender (price or cost, life-cycle cost, price-quality ratio). Exclusion grounds (art. 57): convictions for corruption, fraud, etc., plus discretionary grounds; self-cleaning is possible. Confidentiality (art. 21): mark real trade secrets. Preliminary market consultations are allowed (art. 40) but a vendor who helped prepare the tender must not distort competition (art. 41).
- **Remedies:** a separate directive (89/665/EEC, consolidated 17/04/2014, read 15/11/2026) sets the challenge system: art. 2a standstill of at least 10 calendar days after the award decision is sent electronically (15 days if sent by other means, or 10 days from receipt); art. 2b derogations; art. 2c time limits; a review request by the contracting authority suspends the contract for at least 10 or 15 days after the reply. National transposition differs.
- **Single procurement document (ESPD)** is the standard self-declaration of eligibility.

## 4. United States, FAR basics (read from acquisition.gov)
- **Policy:** contracting officers must promote full and open competition, except in limited listed cases (FAR 6.1; statutory basis cited there).
- **Exchanges with industry before proposals** are encouraged (FAR 15.201) as long as procurement integrity rules are respected: industry days, draft RFPs, one-on-one meetings are legitimate channels, always with equal information to all interested parties.
- **Procurement integrity (FAR 3.104):** nobody may knowingly disclose or obtain contractor bid or proposal information or source selection information before award; employment discussions with an official in the procurement trigger rules; there are post-employment compensation bans for certain former officials. Gifts and gratuities rules are in part 3 as well.
- **Simplified procedures:** micro-purchases and simplified acquisition (Part 13) have thresholds that are inflation-adjusted; check FAR 2.101 for the current figure. FAR is under an ongoing rewrite ("overhaul"); check the current text.
- Small-business set-asides, past performance, and responsiveness to the solicitation drive evaluation.

## 5. What a vendor can and cannot do
| Can | Cannot |
|---|---|
| Read the notice, ask written questions through the official channel, challenge the edital within the deadline | Contact evaluators, pregoeiro or the technical team outside the official channel once the notice is published ("closed period") |
| Take part in public hearings, market consultations, industry days open to all; give technical input that is shared with everyone | Shape the specification privately so that only your product fits (can amount to directing the tender) |
| Seek clarification on ambiguous requirements; propose a technically equivalent alternative if the notice allows | Offer gifts, hospitality, jobs or favours to officials; lobby decision makers about a live tender |
| Form a consortium or subcontract if the notice allows | Coordinate prices or bids with competitors (bid rigging, collusion; see OECD Guidelines for Fighting Bid Rigging, 2009, substantially revised in 2025), exchange information about other bids |
| Mark trade secrets; keep a record of every communication | Submit false documents, inflated references, or CVs of people who will not work on the contract |
| Present products and the vendor's capabilities to the public sector **before** a tender exists, in the open, in a documented way | Promise to the buyer outside the contract (side deals) or accept an instruction that changes the contract without a formal amendment |
Equal treatment is the core rule: whatever you tell one party that is not public information may need to be shared with all. If a buyer contacts you privately about a live tender, say you will answer only through the official channel and log it.

## 6. Reading an edital (checklist)
1. **Object and lots:** exactly what is bought, quantities, delivery place, lot structure (one lot or per item).
2. **Eligibility and qualification:** legal form, tax and social-security clearances, financial ratios, technical capacity (certificates of past work, minimum quantities, team), registrations, insurance. Missing document = disqualification.
3. **Evaluation:** lowest price, price and quality weights, technical scoring method, tie-break rules, preferences for small firms.
4. **Dates:** (count deadlines back from the opening date in working days, skipping weekends and local holidays; example: 3 working days before Thursday 10 Dec is Monday 7 Dec; ask questions a week earlier than the limit) publication, clarification and challenge deadlines (counted back from opening), submission, opening session, validity of bid (often 60-120 days), contract term, delivery milestones, payment term.
5. **Price rules:** reference price, maximum acceptable, unit or global, indexation, taxes included, payment in how many days after acceptance, penalties and guarantees.
6. **Red flags:** a named brand without "or equivalent"; a mandatory site visit on short notice; experience required in quantities far above the object (Lei 14.133 art. 67 par. 2 allows minimum quantities in attestations only up to 50% of the relevant parts); a single narrow certificate; very short delivery; unusual penalties; contradictory clauses; a reference price below real cost. Red flags are grounds for a clarification or a challenge in the window, in writing and neutral in tone.
7. **Bid/no-bid** (decide in 24-48 h): can you meet every mandatory requirement with documents today? Prepared cost vs win probability; margin after the reference price, payment delay and penalties; strategic value of the buyer; can you deliver at the price you would quote. No-bid is a valid, cheaper answer.

## 7. Answering clarifications and writing the response
- Ask: one question per paragraph, cite the clause number, state the ambiguity, propose a reading ("We understand that X; please confirm"). Do not argue the buyer's need. Answers are published to everyone.
- Answer only what is asked, in the order and numbering of the notice; build a compliance matrix (requirement, response, evidence, page). Evidence beats adjectives: certificates, dated references, test reports, named staff with CVs, signed declarations. Remove anything you cannot prove.
- Price exactly as the schedule requires: every line, taxes, arithmetic checked twice; do not leave a line blank or bundle what must be itemised.
- Submit at least a day early; keep the proof of submission. Check file format, signatures, validity.
- After: debrief, appeal within the legal deadline if there are grounds, record why you lost.

## 8. Pricing for public buyers
- Start from your cost (direct, indirect, taxes, payment delay financing, penalties risk, guarantee cost) plus a margin that survives: payment is slow, scope disputes are slower. Compute the unit price at the volumes of the notice; check whether quantities are minimum, estimated or maximum.
- In reverse auctions you bid down in real time: decide your floor price before the session, the lowest at which you still deliver without loss, and do not go below it. Prices far below the estimate may be treated as unrealistic and need justification; bidding at a loss to "buy" the buyer often leads to defaulted delivery and sanctions.
- Registered price lists: price the tail risk (order volumes unknown, price held for the term, no indexation unless allowed).
- Never agree to side payments, rebates to staff or "consultancy fees" connected to the award.

## 9. After award
Contract changes need formal amendments; keep an audit trail of requests and approvals; invoice per the contract. Delivery quality creates references and positions you for the next notice. Escalate disputes through the formal route.
