# Style: B2G tender

## When to use
A public buyer buys through a published procedure (edital, notice, solicitation). Rules, dates and the official channel decide; rapport does not.

## Core moves
1. Read the whole notice and annexes; decide bid or no-bid in 24-48 hours against eligibility, references and margin.
2. Put every question in writing through the official channel; use the challenge window for restrictive clauses.
3. Build a compliance matrix: each requirement, your answer, the proof, the page.
4. Price from cost with a floor you will not cross in a live auction.
5. Submit early; keep proof of submission.
6. Before a notice exists, open and documented market engagement is legitimate; private shaping of a live tender is not.

## Sample talk track (restated; a written clarification)
"Clause 4.2 requires three references of at least X value. Please confirm whether references from subcontracted work count and whether a consortium may combine references. Please publish the answer to all bidders."

## Avoid
- Contact with evaluators outside the channel; gifts; coordination with competitors; false documents.
- Assuming last year's thresholds or rules still apply.
