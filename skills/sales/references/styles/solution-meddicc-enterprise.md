# Style: solution selling and MEDDICC-led enterprise

## When to use
Large deals with a buying group, procurement, security review and a long cycle (often 3-12 months). The risk is not the pitch but missing facts: who decides, how, by when, and why now.

## Core moves
1. Solution selling: start from a diagnosed business problem, not a product; the offer is a solution to a pain the buyer owns and has priced.
2. Run MEDDICC as a question list: Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion, Competition. Score each 0-3 with evidence (`../playbooks.md` 2).
3. Test the champion: do they have access to the economic buyer and a reason to win? Ask them for a meeting with that person.
4. Write a mutual plan with dated steps for both sides: legal, security, pilot, budget, signature.
5. Multi-thread early (three or more contacts across roles) so one departure does not kill the deal.
6. Forecast only deals with a 2 or 3 on Economic buyer, Pain and Decision process.

## Sample talk track (restated)
"To plan this with you rather than guess, may I confirm four things? Who signs, and what do they need to see? What are the steps after the pilot, and how long does your security review usually take? What would make you stop? Could we put those dates into a shared plan today and agree who checks each one?"

## Avoid
- Treating a checklist as a score you trust blindly; blanks are the work plan.
- Calling someone a champion who has no power or no motive.
- Skipping the loser's question: why would they stay with the status quo?
