# Style: founder-led sales

## When to use
The first 20-50 customers, before a repeatable pitch exists. The founder sells to learn: which buyers, which problem, which words, what price holds.

## Core moves
1. Pick a narrow segment you can reach and win; do not sell to everyone.
2. Ask about past behaviour, not opinions: what did you do last time, what did it cost, what have you tried? (Mom Test.)
3. Ask for a real commitment (a paid pilot, a date, an introduction), not compliments.
4. Log every call: objection, words used, price reaction, outcome. Review weekly.
5. Charge from the start, close to the intended price; free pilots teach little about willingness to pay.
6. Hire sales only after about 10 similar closes and a written pitch someone else can run.

## Sample talk track (restated)
"I am not pitching yet; I am trying to learn how you handle X today. Last time it came up, what did you do? What did it cost you? What did you try that failed? If we could solve that, would you pilot it at [price] starting next month, and who else would need to agree?"

## Avoid
- Mistaking polite interest for demand.
- Discounting to get a logo; note the reason if you must.
- Leading questions that hand the buyer the answer.
