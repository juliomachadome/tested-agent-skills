# Style: inbound, self-serve assist and product-led sales

## When to use
Low to mid ticket, card-ready buyers who try the product before talking to anyone. People add value at moments where the product alone stalls: setup, a team rollout, a procurement question.

## Core moves
1. The product and the pricing page carry the sale; no call is required before a card form.
2. Define a product-qualified signal (for example a team invite, hitting a plan limit, a second workspace) and reach out once, with a useful offer, not a pitch.
3. Reply fast to inbound: within an hour in business hours; start from what the person did in the product.
4. Offer help with the blocker (data import, security questionnaire, invoice terms), not a demo of what they already saw.
5. Hand over to a sales-assisted motion only when deal size and complexity justify a human.
6. Measure activation, conversion by signal, and time to first value before adding staff.

## Sample talk track (restated)
"I saw your team created three projects and invited two colleagues, so I will keep this short. If useful, I can help set up the import and answer any security questions your IT team has. If you would rather carry on alone, nothing changes; the guide is here. Want a 15-minute call, or should I just send the checklist?"

## Avoid
- Calls forced before value; gating the product behind a form.
- Outreach on signals that are not lawful to use (consent, tracking rules).
- Treating a trial sign-up as intent to buy.
