## Email standards and mailbox-provider rules
9. Google. *Email sender guidelines* (Google Workspace Admin Help). https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126 **P** 18/11/2026. OFFICIAL. Read: threshold above 5,000 messages a day to Gmail; SPF, DKIM, DMARC; DKIM 1024 bits minimum, 2048 recommended; one-click unsubscribe headers; spam rate below 0.30%, aim below 0.10%; TLS; PTR records. The page gives no unsubscribe processing time. Supports: SKILL 6, `playbooks.md` 7.
10. Yahoo. *Sender best practices* (Yahoo Sender Hub). https://senders.yahooinc.com/best-practices/ **P** 18/11/2026. OFFICIAL. Read: SPF or DKIM for all, both for bulk; DMARC at least p=none for bulk; one-click unsubscribe; spam rate below 0.3%; unsubscribes honoured within 2 days; no volume threshold defined on the page.
11. Levine, J., Herkula, T. *RFC 8058: Signaling One-Click Functionality for List Email Headers*. IETF, 2017. https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc8058 **P** 18/11/2026 (first 100,000 characters). OFFICIAL. Read: List-Unsubscribe-Post header, one HTTPS POST, DKIM must cover the headers, no redirect.
12. Kitterman, S. *RFC 7208: Sender Policy Framework*. IETF, 2014. https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7208 **P** 18/11/2026 (first 100,000 characters). OFFICIAL. Read: result values; limit of 10 DNS-lookup terms; permerror if exceeded.
13. Crocker, D., Hansen, T., Kucherawy, M. *RFC 6376: DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM) Signatures*. IETF, 2011. https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6376 **P** 18/11/2026 (first 100,000 characters). OFFICIAL. Read: signed hashes, selectors, public key in DNS.
14. Kucherawy, M., Zwicky, E. *RFC 7489: DMARC*. IETF (Independent Submission, Informational), 2015. https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7489 **P** 18/11/2026 (first 100,000 characters). OFFICIAL. Read: policies none, quarantine, reject; alignment; rua and ruf. The page lists later documents as obsoleting it (RFC 9989 to 9991); their text was not opened, so cite behaviour at the level of this entry only.
15. Apple. *Apple advances its privacy leadership with iOS 15* (newsroom, 2021). https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2021/06/apple-advances-its-privacy-leadership-with-ios-15-ipados-15-macos-monterey-and-watchos-8/ **P** 18/11/2026. OFFICIAL. Read: Mail Privacy Protection blocks hidden tracking images and masks the IP address. Supports: open rates unreliable (SKILL 9).
16. Resend. *Domains* (documentation). https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/domains/introduction **P** 18/11/2026. VENDOR. Read: verify an owned domain, send from a subdomain to isolate reputation, tracking set per domain. The page did not cover SPF or DKIM records.
17. Klaviyo. *Understanding message conversion tracking* (Help Center). https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005248128 **P** 18/11/2026. VENDOR. Read: attribution windows and models, Apple MPP can affect opens. The page did not cover flows or smart sending.

## Law and consumer rules (examples; check the recipient's country)
18. European Parliament and Council. *Directive 2002/58/EC (ePrivacy), art. 13*. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32002L0058 **P** 18/11/2026. OFFICIAL. Read: prior consent for email marketing, existing-customer exception, no disguised sender. Member-state law differs.
19. US Federal Trade Commission. *CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business*. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business **P** 18/11/2026. OFFICIAL. Read: header and subject accuracy, ad identification, postal address, opt-out honoured within 10 business days, per-email penalty (amount as stated on the page, indexed yearly), transactional exception.
20. Brasil. *Lei 13.709/2018 (LGPD)*. https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm **P** 18/11/2026 (first 100,000 of 125,310 characters; arts. 7, 8, 18, 52 read). OFFICIAL. Read: legal bases, consent form and proof, revocation, rights, fine cap 2% of Brazilian revenue and R$50 million per violation.

## Channel and platform rules
21. Apple. *App Review Guidelines*, 4.5.4. https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ **P** 18/11/2026 (first 100,000 of 114,384 characters). OFFICIAL. Read: push must not be required for core function, no sensitive data, promotional push only with explicit in-app consent and an opt-out.
22. Android Developers. *Notification runtime permission*. https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/notification-permission **P** 18/11/2026. OFFICIAL. Read: POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13 and above, off by default for new installs, request in context after the user knows the app.
23. OneSignal. *Prompt for push permissions* (docs). https://documentation.onesignal.com/docs/en/prompt-for-push-permissions **P** 18/11/2026. VENDOR. Read: soft prompt before the native prompt, avoid first launch, iOS denial not re-promptable, Android has a second attempt.
24. WhatsApp. *Business Messaging Policy*. https://whatsappbusiness.com/pt-br/policy/ **P** 18/11/2026 (page in Portuguese URL, content read in English summary). OFFICIAL. Read: opt-in, honour opt-outs, prohibited content, blocks and reports can limit sending, templates outside the 24-hour customer-service window, human escalation for automation.
25. Meta. *WhatsApp template fundamentals*. https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview **P** 18/11/2026. OFFICIAL. Read: categories authentication, marketing, utility; review of templates; paused and disabled states. Window duration and pricing are on linked pages that were not read.

26. Meta. *WhatsApp Business Platform pricing* (developers page). https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing **P** 18/11/2026. OFFICIAL. Read: a 24-hour customer-service window opens when a user messages or calls the business and restarts with each new user message; inside it the business may send free-form messages, but only as replies; outside it, only template messages; templates can also be used inside the window; template categories are marketing, utility and authentication. Price amounts were not read.

27. UK. *Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003*, reg. 22. https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/regulation/22 . **P** 18/11/2026 (browser pass): marketing email to individual subscribers needs prior consent; soft opt-in exception when details came from a sale or negotiation, for the sender's similar products only, with a free refusal offered at collection and in every message; separate charity exception.
28. US. *47 CFR 64.1200* (FCC rules implementing the TCPA). eCFR API, version 2026-11-01. **P** 18/11/2026: autodialed or prerecorded telemarketing calls to mobile numbers need prior express written consent (signed agreement with clear disclosure); "call" includes SMS; telephone solicitations not before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. recipient local time; national do-not-call registry must be honoured.
