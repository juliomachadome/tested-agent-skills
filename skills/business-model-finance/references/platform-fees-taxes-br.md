Merchant of record (Gumroad, Paddle, app stores) is the seller of record and handles sales tax and VAT; a processor (Stripe) does not. Their percentages look higher because they include tax compliance **[C]**. Compare net after tax work, not the headline rate.

How to use: fee per sale = p x price + f. At R$97, Kiwify is R$11.21 (11.6%), Eduzz Basic R$7.24 (7.5%). Add instalment, instant-payout and currency fees if any. Date every number in your sheet.

## 2. MEI (Microempreendedor Individual)
Status S: the Portal do Empreendedor pages opened this pass need a browser; the statutory text (Lei Complementar 123/2006) was not read. Confirm at gov.br/empresas-e-negocios.
- Annual revenue limit commonly stated as R$81,000 (R$6,750 per month, proportional in the opening year) with a tolerance of up to 20% over (pay the difference on the DAS); more than 20% over means leaving MEI retroactively **[S]**. Revenue is gross (before platform fees) **[S]**.
- One employee at most; activities restricted to an allowed-CNAE list; many intellectual or regulated professions are not allowed **[S]**. A product made of services (courses, coaching, software) needs checking against the official list.
- Monthly fixed payment (DAS-MEI) plus a small ICMS/ISS component; annual declaration (DASN-SIMEI) **[S]**.
Example: a course selling 300 units at R$97 is R$29,100 a month, about R$349,000 a year: far above the limit.

## 3. Simples Nacional, annexes, Fator R
Status S (official site opened, tables not read). Confirm at the Receita Federal and Simples Nacional portals.
- Annual revenue ceiling stated as R$4.8 million; tax is a percentage of revenue by bracket and annex; five annexes by activity **[S]**.
- Service companies fall in Annex III (lower rates) or Annex V (higher) depending on Fator R, payroll including pro-labore over the last 12 months divided by revenue; threshold 28% **[S]**. Small founders often tune pro-labore to cross it: ask the accountant for the real net effect (INSS and income tax on the pro-labore).
- Software and education activities have specific rules; do not assume the annex.

## 4. Invoices and accountant
- Platforms that are the seller of record may issue the consumer invoice; if you sell directly, check whether you must issue a nota fiscal de serviço (NFS-e, municipal) or NF-e (goods) **[S]**.
- Hire an accountant when: you approach the MEI limit, you plan a company (ME, LTDA), you hire, you sell abroad, or the monthly bill passes the accountant's cost. Ask: which regime pays least net, which activity code, how to receive foreign income, what invoices to issue.

## 5. EU VAT on digital services (One Stop Shop)
Status P for the European Commission OSS page (taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/business/vat/oss_en): digital services (telecommunications, broadcasting and electronic services) to EU consumers are taxed where the customer is; since 1 July 2021 an EU-wide threshold of EUR 10,000 applies to cross-border supplies by suppliers established in the EU, and the OSS lets a business declare and pay VAT for all EU states in one return **[C]**. Status S: the rules for non-EU sellers (no threshold, non-Union OSS registration) were not read this pass; check the same page and a local adviser. Selling through a merchant of record (Gumroad, Paddle, app stores) removes most of this work. Keep customer location evidence (billing country, IP) for audits.

**Verified 18/11/2026 in the law itself** (LC 123/2006 consolidated text, bibliography entry 12): MEI limit R$ 81.000 per year (art. 18-A § 1º); small business (EPP) R$ 360.000 to R$ 4.800.000 (art. 3º II); Fator R threshold 28% payroll/revenue moves listed services to Annex III (art. 18 §§ 5º-I and 5º-J). Rates and limits change by new complementary laws: re-check before advising, and confirm with an accountant for a specific case.
