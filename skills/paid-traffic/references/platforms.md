# How the platforms deliver: official vs folklore

Read on 13/11/2026 unless noted. **[search]** = from search summaries; the official page did not open fully (Meta Help Center and Transparency Center often return only the title to automated reading): verify in the dashboard before treating as a hard rule.

## Meta (Facebook, Instagram)
- **[C] Optimises for the chosen event and allocates by the cost of the next conversion, not the average** ("Breakdown Effect", Meta Help Center via Jon Loomer). Evaluate at the level where budget is divided (campaign for campaign budget; ad set for automatic placements or several ads). Questioning the allocation is legitimate when the aggregate is bad.
- **[C] Learning phase:** an ad set exits after about 50 optimisation events in the week after the last significant edit; otherwise "learning limited". Shops ads need at least 17 purchases on the website and 5 on Meta. [Help Center, read 15/11/2026]
- **[M] Edits that reset learning** (table attributed to the Help Center, via a secondary source): campaign budget increase above ~20%, bid value/strategy; ad-set audience, placements, optimisation event, **adding a new ad**, bid; any creative, text or URL change. Practical consequence: new creatives in a weekly batch, not drip-fed.
- **[F] "Never increase more than 20%" is not law.** Documentation is vague and practitioners report more tolerance for budget increases on performing ad sets. What does not change: marginal cost rises with budget.
- **[F] "Learning limited" on small budgets is normal,** a label, not a defect; do not restructure only to clear the warning. What matters is CPA in your backend.
- **[C] Unified sales campaigns.** Legacy Advantage+ Shopping and App campaigns were deprecated in the API during 2026 (creation and editing blocked from v25; remaining ones paused around v26). Sales, Leads and App setups now start "automation first"; the existing-customer budget cap in that format is gone. Guides from 2023-2025 telling you to cap it at 20-30% are outdated. (Meta for Developers blog, 13/02/2026)
- **[M] With Advantage+ audience, only some fields are controls:** location, minimum age (up to 25), language, excluded custom audiences. Maximum age, gender, interests and lookalikes become suggestions. Interest exclusions were removed in 2024. [search; check in the creation screen]
- **[C] Andromeda is the retrieval stage, not ranking:** it narrows tens of millions of candidate ads to thousands. Meta's own figures: model capacity 10,000× larger, +6% recall, +8% ad quality in selected segments; no independent numbers. (Meta Engineering, 02/12/2024)
- **[M] What changed:** Meta now talks about "diversifying creative" instead of narrow targeting. Different means another concept, format or persona, not the same piece with a new colour. The old "max six ads per ad set" left the docs. Volume alone guarantees nothing.
- **[X] "A similarity score above 60% suppresses the ad"** and **"50 ads per ad set is the new standard"**: no Meta source; the second is one practitioner's experiment.
- **[M] Attribution:** default optimisation window 7-day click, 1-day view. On 12/01/2026 the 7- and 28-day view windows left Ads Manager and the API; some reports say "click" now requires a link click. No official notice found. Always compare the "click only" column with your backend. [search]
- **[C] Conversions API, as Meta asks:** use alongside the pixel with the same events; deduplicate with identical `event_name` and `event_id`; send `client_user_agent` on every web event and as many customer parameters as law and consent allow (`em`, `external_id`, `fbp`, `fbc`; IP improves matching); send close to event time; truthful `action_source`. Event match quality shows in Events Manager (0-10). The page does not cover consent: that is the sender's duty. (Meta for Developers, "Conversions API best practices")
- **[C] Meta creative testing tool:** runs inside a highest-volume-bid campaign (no cost cap, bid cap or ROAS goal), tests 2-5 new ads with similar spend, on a budget slice (Meta suggests up to 20%), 7 days by default. Afterwards Meta allocates as it likes and may not favour the winner. Size it so the best ad gets near 50 events, and test truly different pieces. (Jon Loomer)
- **[M] Fatigue labels:** "creative limited" or "creative fatigue" when the audience saw the piece too often and cost per result rises versus earlier ads (most severe label from about double). An alert, not a sentence. [search]
- **[C][EU] Transparency and consent:** every ad targeting the EU declares a **beneficiary** and **payer**, public; the Ad Library keeps all EU ads for a year with dates, targeting parameters and reach. Since January 2026 EU users can choose "less personalised ads" (DMA commitment); no reliable public number on the effect. Political and social-issue ads stopped running in the EU on 06/10/2025. [search for the last two]
- **[M] Personal attributes:** ads may not assert or imply knowledge of personal characteristics (race, religion, age, health, financial status, etc.). "You" is fine; tying "you" to a listed attribute is not. A situation question passes; "You're 50 and…" hits age. [search]
- **[F] Incrementality: the dashboard errs both ways.** A measurement vendor reports 640 experiments (2024-2025) from its large clients: Meta drove on average 19% lift on the primary KPI; 7-day-click reporting **under**-stated incremental direct sales by ~15%; Advantage+ beat manual mid-test and lost at the end, and 58% of brands had higher incremental return with manual; top-of-funnel campaigns looked worse in-platform and were more incremental. Self-selected big advertisers: a warning that the dashboard is not truth, not a number to expect. (Haus, "The Meta Report") [1-SRC]

## TikTok
- **[C] Learning:** volatility drops after about 25 campaign results or 7 days; do not pause or edit mid-way; keep budget and creative count reasonable. The "needs 50 conversions" repeated by blogs is not on that page. (TikTok Ads Help, "Learning phase")
- **[C] Smart+ (upgraded):** one flow where each module (audience, budget, placement, creative) can be automatic or manual; up to 50 asset groups each with its own URL and up to 50 creatives; combines videos and texts. For Sales, Leads, Traffic and App; not yet in all accounts.
- **[C] Spark Ads:** use an organic post (yours or a creator's, with an authorisation code) as an ad; engagement stays on the original post after the campaign. No official performance numbers.
- **[F] Minimum budget** figures in blogs (20/50/100 USD per day per group) have no platform source: use the CPA math with 25 results in 7 days.
- **[F] When it makes sense:** after Meta breaks even and at least three videos have sold, ideally already published organically to run as Spark. Before that, it splits a budget too small for one channel.

## Google and YouTube
- **[C] Demand Gen replaced Video Action campaigns:** creation blocked April 2025, automatic migration July 2025 to April 2026. Inventory: YouTube (in-stream, in-feed, Shorts), Discover, Gmail, Display, with channel controls.
- **[C] Google's own orders of magnitude for that migration:** daily budget above 20× expected CPA on conversion bidding; wait 2 weeks or 50 conversions before changing; bid adjustments of at most 15% with a week between them; merge groups with under ~30 conversions in 30 days; tests of 4-6 weeks. (Google Ads Help)
- **[MATH] Consequence for low ticket:** with CPA 50, Google's rule asks for more than 1,000 per day on a video campaign. A scaling-phase channel, not a validation one.
- **[F] Search is the cheap exception:** captures existing demand ("online course in X", "X level test") and protects the brand; volume capped by searches, course clicks are contested. Size with the country's Keyword Planner first.
- **[F] Performance Max without a catalogue** mixes brand, search and display in ways hard to read in a small account; not a first choice without conversion history.
- **[M][EU] Google requires Consent Mode v2** for personalisation in the EU and measures better with enhanced conversions (hashed e-mail), always under marketing consent. (not re-read)
