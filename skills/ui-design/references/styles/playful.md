# Style: playful

## Principles
Warmth, reward and personality for consumer products: rounded shapes, saturated colour, friendly type, illustration or mascots, expressive motion on success moments. The play lives in the edges (empty states, celebrations, onboarding, illustrations), while the core task stays clear. M3 Expressive pursues a similar goal inside a system: bigger and more varied shapes, emphasized type, springy motion for hero moments, with a standard scheme for utilitarian parts [C, M3 Motion; M3 Type scale emphasized styles].

## Typical tokens
| Area | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | rounded or friendly sans for headings (weights 600 to 800), a calm sans for body at 16 to 18 px; sentence case; short, kind copy |
| Colour | 1 strong brand colour plus 2 to 3 supporting hues, tinted surfaces; each hue has a text-safe darker variant; status colours stay separate |
| Space | 4 or 8 base, roomy: 16, 24, 32 |
| Radius | 16 to 28 px on cards, pill buttons, circular avatars |
| Shadow | soft coloured shadows or flat colour blocks; optional chunky outline |
| Motion | springy, 200 to 400 ms, small overshoot for rewards; calm fade for errors and forms |
| Targets | large: 48 to 56 px for kids and one-hand use |

## Do
- Reward the right moments (task done, streak) and keep errors calm and specific.
- Use illustration to explain empty states and onboarding, not to fill space.
- Keep one primary button, large and obvious.
- Make characters and colours carry meaning consistently.

## Avoid
- Confetti on every action, or celebration for failure states.
- White text on bright yellow, orange or pastel fills (check each pair; #F5B700 on white is 1.8:1).
- Cute copy where the user is anxious: payments, health, errors, deletion.
- Bouncy motion in forms and tables.

## Accessibility risks
Saturated brand colours rarely give 4.5:1 with white (orange #FF6B35 on white is 2.84, white on #FF6B6B is 2.78); round typefaces with ambiguous letterforms (I, l, 1); motion and sound overload; colour-only game states; small decorative text on illustrations.

## Fits / does not fit
Fits: learning, kids, habit, wellness and social apps, food, consumer fintech onboarding, games. Does not fit: legal, enterprise admin, critical safety flows, dense data.
