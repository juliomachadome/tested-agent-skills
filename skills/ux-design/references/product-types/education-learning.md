# Education and learning

## Key jobs
A learner understands, practices and remembers, and sees progress honestly; a teacher or author builds and tracks content without extra work. Motivation is fragile: friction in the first sessions loses people.

## Critical flows
Placement or goal setting; first lesson; lesson, exercise and feedback loop; review and practice scheduling; progress and certificates; teacher authoring and grading; payment and access.

## Typical pitfalls
- Long setup before the first lesson; level tests with no escape; tours rather than a first exercise.
- Feedback that says wrong without saying why or how to fix; exercises that give away the answer; time pressure without need.
- Progress bars that reset, or streaks that punish illness (gamification limits in `games-gamified.md`).
- Dense reading with no chunks, no examples, small text; audio and video without captions and transcripts.
- Dependence on color for right and wrong (WCAG 1.4.1); timers with no extension (2.2.1).
- Course content structure that mirrors the author's outline, not the learner's questions (labels, IA).
- Leaderboards or public scores that shame beginners; no private mode.
- Teacher dashboards that list data but not the next student to help.
- Deadline and progress dark patterns ("only 2 seats left" on a course).

## Metrics
Time to first completed exercise; lesson completion; return to next lesson within 48 hours; accuracy trend; time per exercise; review adherence; completion at course end; learner-reported confidence (SEQ); teacher time to publish a lesson. Be careful reading completion as learning: it measures finishing, not understanding.

## Checklist
- [ ] First exercise reachable within the first minutes without signing up where possible.
- [ ] Feedback states the correct answer, the reason, and what to do next.
- [ ] Progress is honest and persistent; missed days do not erase it.
- [ ] Text, audio and video alternatives; adjustable speed; no color-only correctness.
- [ ] Time limits adjustable (2.2.1); breaks allowed; work saved.
- [ ] Teacher flows: authoring autosave, preview as learner, bulk grading with undo.
- [ ] Certificates and claims are true and verifiable.

## Worked check
Sit with three new learners and time how long until each completes a first exercise; the gap between the fastest and slowest shows where setup, not content, is the barrier.
