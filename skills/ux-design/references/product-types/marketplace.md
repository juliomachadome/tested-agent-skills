# Marketplace (two-sided)

## Key jobs
Buyers find a trustworthy match quickly and know the full price; sellers list easily, get found, get paid on time and handle disputes fairly. The platform keeps both sides and keeps them on the platform.

## Critical flows
Seller onboarding and listing creation; search, filters and ranking; listing detail (photos, price, availability, reviews); messaging; booking or checkout; payout; reviews; disputes and refunds; identity and safety.

## Typical pitfalls
- Cold start: empty search results with no alternatives (Baymard no-results strategies); sellers with no feedback after listing.
- Hidden fees revealed late; price on the card differs from price at checkout.
- Review systems that look gamed; no way to report or reply; fake urgency ("3 people viewing").
- Listing forms too long for sellers, no draft saving, errors after the final step.
- Messaging with no notifications settings, leading to ignored buyers or notification fatigue.
- Dispute flows buried; unclear who decides; the same state labeled differently for buyer and seller (one name per thing).
- Disclosure of sponsored ranking missing.
- Trust signals (verified badge, response rate) with no explanation of what they mean or how they are earned.
- Sellers cannot see why a listing ranks low, so they game it.

## Metrics
Search-to-contact and contact-to-booking rates; zero-result rate; listing completion rate and time; seller response time; payout time; dispute rate and resolution time; repeat rate per side; tickets per 1,000 transactions by side. Measure each side separately; a blended number hides a failing side.

## Checklist
- [ ] Two journeys mapped; the same event has the same name on both sides.
- [ ] All-in price visible from the result card to checkout.
- [ ] Zero-result and low-supply states propose next steps.
- [ ] Listing form: autosave, photo upload error handling, preview before publish.
- [ ] Review display shows count, date, and how verified; report route exists.
- [ ] Dispute path reachable in two taps from the order; deadlines and outcomes stated.
- [ ] Sponsored or boosted results labeled.
- [ ] Messages keep context (listing, dates) and have notification controls.

## Worked check
Take one order from each side and write the state names each sees at every step (paid, in transit, delivered, disputed). If one event has two names, or a state is visible to one side only, fix the glossary first.

## Sources
