# Games and gamified products

## Key jobs
A player feels competent, in control and rewarded fairly; a gamified product (fitness, learning, finance) uses game elements to support a goal the person already has. Fun is the product in a game; in a gamified product, the person's own goal is.

## Critical flows
First-time user experience and tutorial; core loop; progression (levels, unlocks, rewards); social features and leaderboards; store and monetization; settings, accessibility and parental controls; return after a break.

## Typical pitfalls
- Long tutorials before play; text walls; tutorials that cannot be skipped or replayed.
- Streak and loss-aversion pressure, fake scarcity and timed offers, confusing currencies hiding real prices, pressure on children: deceptive or addictive patterns (deceptive.design lists addictive design; Mathur et al. on urgency and scarcity). Legal rules vary by market; ask counsel.
- Rewards for the metric, not the goal (badges for logging in, not for learning); leaderboards that demotivate most people.
- Inaccessible play: color-only cues, no remapping, no subtitles, motion and flashing without a reduced option (WCAG 2.3.1 flashing, 2.2.2).
- Controls too small on touch; HUD obscuring the play; no pause.
- Monetization before the player has any value.

## Metrics
Tutorial completion and time to first fun moment; day-1, day-7 and day-30 retention; session length and sessions per day, read next to well-being and complaint signals; progression funnel; reward redemption; spend and refund rate with complaint themes; accessibility option use. A retention lift from a pressure mechanic is not a clean win.

## Checklist
- [ ] First play within a minute or two; tutorial skippable and replayable.
- [ ] Rewards tie to the person's real goal; progress is never lost by an absence.
- [ ] Real price visible beside every virtual currency; spending limits available.
- [ ] No fake counters or timers; offers honest and symmetrical to decline.
- [ ] Remappable controls, captions, color-independent cues, reduced motion, no flashing above WCAG limits.
- [ ] Pause and save anywhere; targets at least 44 px on touch.
- [ ] Children's modes and consent reviewed by the owner.
